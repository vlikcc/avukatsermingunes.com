The Turkish green passport, called “hususi damgalı pasaport” (special stamped passport) in the law, is issued only to the people listed in Article 14 of Passport Law No. 5682 (Pasaport Kanunu) and in a few special laws: senior public officials and those who retired from such posts, mayors, lawyers with at least fifteen years of practice, executives of certain exporting companies, and the spouses and children of these people. Doctors, engineers, architects, pharmacists, accountants and village heads have no such right today. The bills that would add them were still before a committee of the Turkish parliament in November 2026.

On 7 November 2026 the search “10 meslek grubuna yeşil pasaport” (green passport for ten professions) was among the most searched terms in Turkey. Below you will find what the law says today, the official status of the bills, and what matters if you live in Germany, the Netherlands or elsewhere in Europe.

## Turkish green passport: who is eligible under the law?

Article 12 of the Passport Law lists five kinds of passport: diplomatic, special stamped (hususi damgalı), service stamped (hizmet damgalı), ordinary (umuma mahsus) and passports for foreigners. The “green passport” is the special stamped passport. Article 14(A) lists who can receive it. Under the text in force in November 2026 they are:

- civil servants and other public officials in first, second and third grade posts (including contract staff counted against those posts),
- professors at foundation universities with at least 15 years of professional seniority,
- mayors who earned a pension from a first grade post,
- people from these groups who left office through retirement or resignation,
- provincial and district mayors, for their term of office,
- lawyers registered with a bar and with at least fifteen years of seniority (see below),
- executives of companies whose average annual exports over the last three years exceed a value set by the President, for four years, provided they have not been convicted of the offences listed in the law.

Some other laws refer to Article 14(A). For example, Article 8(1) of Law No. 5774 gives a special passport, on application, to athletes who receive an honorary pension and hold the title of State Athlete.

The paragraph on lawyers was added in 2019 by Law No. 7188. It comes with conditions: there must be no investigation or prosecution against the lawyer for the offences in Book Two, Part Four, Chapters Four to Seven of the Turkish Criminal Code (TCK) or under Anti-Terror Law No. 3713. The law says the passport “may be issued”, and the details are set by a regulation of the Ministry of the Interior. This is the provision in Article 14 that grants the special passport to a self-employed profession based on seniority and conditions. Bill No. 2/3200, described below, proposes a similar structure: fifteen years of seniority and no pending investigation or prosecution.

## Green passport for spouses and children

Article 14(A) also covers family members:

- **Spouses** of people entitled to a special passport receive the same passport.
- The widow or widower of a person who was entitled at the time of death may receive it, as long as they have not remarried.
- **Children:** minor children; adult children who live with the holder, are unmarried, have no job and are still in education, **until they turn 25**; and adult children who live with the holder, are unmarried, have no job and need permanent care because of a disability, as certified by an official medical board report.

For adult children all conditions must be met together. A child who marries or starts working drops out, even if still studying.

In the text, the paragraphs on spouses and children come after the paragraph on lawyers and before the paragraph on exporters. Whether the families of export company executives also qualify is not clear from the wording. This point is disputed: I have found no express provision and no binding court decision on it, so the practice should be confirmed with the Ministry of the Interior.

## What does “green passport for 10 professions” mean?

As of November 2026 there is **no** new law giving green passports to professional groups. I checked this in three official sources:

- The current text of the Passport Law in the Ministry of Justice’s UYAP Mevzuat system (recorded 21/11/2024) and the official PDF on mevzuat.gov.tr carry the same date. The last entry in the list of amendments is the Constitutional Court decision of 24/9/2024, E. 2024/112, K. 2024/160, on Article 22. Article 14 was last amended by Law No. 7243 (in force since 17/4/2020).
- The Official Gazette (Resmî Gazete) of 7 November 2026, issue 33423, published no law and nothing on the Passport Law.
- In the records of the Grand National Assembly (TBMM) the relevant bills are listed as “Komisyonda” (in committee).

I looked at two bills on the TBMM website:

- **Bill No. 2/3836** (28th term, 5th legislative year, received 16/09/2026): according to its summary it would allow village heads (muhtar), accountants (mali müşavir), pharmacists, doctors, engineers and architects who meet set criteria to receive a special passport. Lead committee: Interior Affairs Committee (received 01/10/2026). Status: in committee.
- **Bill No. 2/3200** (28th term, 3rd legislative year, received 08/07/2025): it would give a special passport to engineers and architects who are members of a TMMOB chamber with at least fifteen years of seniority, provided there is no investigation or prosecution for certain offences. Lead committee: Interior Affairs Committee (received 09/07/2025). Status: in committee.

I found no bill or official text matching “10 professions”; press reports mention different numbers of professions and different bills. A bill gives no one any right until it is adopted in committee, debated in the plenary, published in the Official Gazette as a law and in force. A bill in committee can change or never pass.

## Where do you apply?

The procedure is in Article 14(C). The special passport is issued by the Ministry of the Interior on the application of the competent superior of the authority the applicant belongs to. Under the same conditions, Turkish embassies and legations may issue it with the consent of the Ministry of the Interior and on the instruction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The same written application is needed for spouses and children.

People who left office through retirement or resignation apply directly to the Ministry of the Interior with documents showing their status. The Ministry sets the validity period, at least six months; an expired passport can be extended five times by embassies and legations, again with the Ministry’s consent and the Foreign Ministry’s instruction.

Which office takes applications in practice today, which documents are required and what it costs is not in the law. Please check the official notices of the General Directorate of Population and Citizenship Affairs (NVİ) and the Turkish missions. I give no amounts here.

## What it means if you live in Europe

The visa advantage of a green passport does not come from Turkish law. Turkish law only decides the type of passport and who gets it. Whether you can enter a country without a visa is up to that country or group of countries.

For the Schengen area the basic rule is Regulation (EU) 2018/1806. Turkey is in its Annex I, the list of countries whose nationals need a visa. Article 6(1)(a) says member states may exempt “holders of diplomatic, service/official or special passports” from the visa requirement. “May” means each member state decides for itself. How Germany, the Netherlands or another Schengen state treats the Turkish special passport today should be checked with that state’s official sources. I do not advise on German or Dutch law; for that you need a lawyer admitted in that country or the competent authority.

If you live in Germany or the Netherlands on a residence permit, your right to live there comes from that permit. A green passport does not replace it. The rights of people who left Turkish citizenship with permission are a separate topic, covered in our [article on the Blue Card (Mavi Kart)](/en/publications/blue-card-turkey-mavi-kart-rights). Other legal matters between the two countries are on the [Türkiye–Germany page](/en/turkiye-germany).

## Frequently asked questions

### Have doctors and engineers been given green passports?

No. As of November 2026, Article 14 of the Passport Law does not list doctors, engineers, architects, pharmacists, accountants or village heads. Bills No. 2/3836 and 2/3200 cover these professions and are both before the TBMM Interior Affairs Committee.

### Do all Turkish civil servants get a green passport?

No. The law covers civil servants and other public officials in first, second and third grade posts, and those of them who left through retirement or resignation.

### Up to what age can children of holders get a green passport?

Minor children qualify. Adult children qualify only if they live with the holder, are unmarried, have no job and are in education, and then until they turn 25. There is no age limit for children with a disability who need permanent care.

### Can you travel to Germany visa-free with a green passport?

That is decided by Regulation (EU) 2018/1806 and German practice, not by Turkish law. The Regulation allows member states to exempt holders of special passports. Check the current position with the official German authorities.

Article 55 MÖHUK, part of Turkey’s Law No. 5718 on Private International Law and International Civil Procedure (Milletlerarası Özel Hukuk ve Usul Hukuku Hakkında Kanun, MÖHUK), sets out how a Turkish court handles an application to enforce a foreign judgment (tenfiz) and what the other side may object to. The application is served together with the hearing date, the court decides under the simple procedure (basit yargılama usulü), and the respondent can raise only the grounds listed in the law, not argue that the foreign judgment was wrong. This matters if you want a German, Dutch or other foreign judgment enforced in Turkey, and equally if such a case has been brought against you.

## Article 55 MÖHUK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **Service and objection**
>
> Article 55 – (1) The application for enforcement shall be served on the other party together with the hearing date. The same applies to the recognition and enforcement of decisions in non-contentious matters. The service rule does not apply to non-contentious decisions that have no opposing party. The application shall be examined and decided under the rules of the simple procedure.
>
> (2) The other party may object only on the grounds that the conditions for enforcement under this section are not met, or that the foreign judgment has been wholly or partly satisfied, or that a reason preventing its satisfaction has arisen.

Turkish original:

> **Tebliğ ve itiraz**
>
> MADDE 55 – (1) Tenfiz istemine ilişkin dilekçe, duruşma günü ile birlikte karşı tarafa tebliğ edilir. İhtilâfsız kaza kararlarının tanınması ve tenfizi de aynı hükme tâbidir. Hasımsız ihtilâfsız kaza kararlarında tebliğ hükmü uygulanmaz. İstem, basit yargılama usulü hükümlerine göre incelenerek karara bağlanır.
>
> (2) Karşı taraf ancak bu bölüm hükümlerine göre tenfiz şartlarının bulunmadığını veya yabancı mahkeme ilâmının kısmen veya tamamen yerine getirilmiş yahut yerine getirilmesine engel bir sebep ortaya çıkmış olduğunu öne sürerek itiraz edebilir.

The article has not changed since 2007. As of November 2026, the amendment table of the current text in the UYAP Mevzuat system lists only Article 27 (amended by Law No. 7550).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the official reasoning, the article partly corresponds to Article 39 of the former Law No. 2675. That provision required service of the application with the hearing date but said nothing about non-contentious decisions (ihtilafsız kaza). To remove doubts in practice, the new text states that service also applies to their recognition and enforcement, and does not apply where the decision has no opposing party (hasımsız). (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, Article 54 of the bill.)

## Explanation

### Service and a hearing are required

The application is served together with the hearing date, not on its own. The Yargıtay (Court of Cassation) applies this strictly: where the court served the application but then granted recognition and enforcement on the papers without holding a hearing, the decision was reversed (see the 2nd Civil Chamber’s 2023 decision below). Article 320(1) of the Turkish Code of Civil Procedure (HMK) does allow a decision on the file where possible in the simple procedure. Because Article 55(1) expressly requires service of the hearing date, that option is not available in cases with an opposing party.

The service rule also covers the recognition and enforcement of non-contentious decisions. Where the decision has no opposing party, there is no one to serve.

### What the simple procedure means

The simple procedure is governed by Articles 316 to 322 HMK. These provisions are unchanged as of November 2026; the UYAP text of the HMK is consolidated up to Law No. 7589 (in force since 31 July 2026), which did not touch them. The rules that matter most in enforcement cases:

- The time limit for the response is two weeks from service. If preparing the response in that time is very difficult or impossible, the court may, on an application made within the two weeks, grant a one-off extension of up to two weeks (Article 317(2) HMK).
- There is no reply or rejoinder (Article 317(3) HMK).
- The parties must list all their evidence with their pleadings and attach the documents they hold (Article 318 HMK).
- The bar on expanding or changing the defence starts when the response is filed (Article 319 HMK).
- Apart from the first hearing, the taking of evidence is to be completed in two hearings, no more than one month apart as a rule (Article 320(3) HMK).

Where the simple procedure has no rule, the rules of the ordinary written procedure apply (Article 322(1) HMK).

### Only limited objections

Paragraph 2 leaves the respondent three kinds of objection: the conditions for enforcement (mainly Articles 50 and 54 MÖHUK) are not met, the judgment has been wholly or partly satisfied, or a reason preventing its satisfaction has arisen. Objections to the merits, for example that the foreign judge weighed the evidence wrongly or set maintenance too high, are not on the list. Objections that the judgment has been paid or that the claim is time-barred, on the other hand, have been examined by the Yargıtay as objections under Article 55(2) (see below).

Some conditions in Article 54 are examined only if the respondent objects: that the judgment was given by a court of a state with no real connection to the dispute (Article 54(1)(b)), and that the respondent was not duly summoned to, or represented before, the foreign court (Article 54(1)(ç)). Since the bar on expanding the defence begins with the response in the simple procedure, these objections should be raised in the response. This is my own reading of how the law fits together; I did not find a Yargıtay decision that settles this point directly.

Article 55 applies by analogy to the enforcement of foreign arbitral awards (Article 61(2) MÖHUK). For the competent court, see [Article 51 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-51-mohuk-competent-court-turkey); for what an enforcement decision does, see [Article 50 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-50-mohuk-enforcement-turkey).

## Practical points if you live abroad

**If you are the respondent.** If enforcement or recognition (tanıma) proceedings have been brought against you in Turkey, the two-week response period runs from service. You must file your objections and evidence (for example proof of payment, documents showing you were not summoned in the foreign proceedings, or a later decision varying the judgment) with the response; there is no second written round.

**If you are the applicant.** For service to be possible, the application must state the other party’s address (Article 52(1)(a) MÖHUK). If the other party lives in Germany, the Netherlands or another country, service takes place abroad. How that is done is governed by the Turkish Law on Service (Tebligat Kanunu) and the treaties to which Turkey is a party; this article does not go into it.

**Recognition of a divorce.** Even if the spouses divorced by agreement abroad, the service requirement still applies in the Turkish recognition case: a divorce judgment has an opposing party. For general information, see [Private international law](/en/practice-areas/private-international-law).

## Yargıtay decisions

**Yargıtay 2. Hukuk Dairesi, E. 2023/3912, K. 2023/5837, T. 30.11.2023**

The applicant sought recognition and enforcement of an Azerbaijani divorce and custody judgment. The first-instance court served the application but decided on the papers without a hearing. The Chamber reversed: the application must be served together with the hearing date, and for a decision that is not non-contentious, deciding without serving the hearing date and without giving the respondent the chance to object breaches procedural law.

**Yargıtay 2. Hukuk Dairesi, E. 2023/4393, K. 2024/3361, T. 13.05.2024**

The case concerned enforcement of a Swiss decision on maintenance arrears; the respondent pleaded limitation. The Chamber held that this objection was raised under Article 55(2) and that the court had to examine it as a preliminary issue and decide accordingly. Granting enforcement merely because the conditions of Articles 54 and 58 were met was wrong, and the decision was reversed.

**Yargıtay 2. Hukuk Dairesi, E. 2023/5595, K. 2023/5966, T. 06.12.2023**

The applicant sought enforcement of an Austrian maintenance judgment. The regional appeal court found that the judgment had been enforced in Austria and that the respondent had made part payments, so his objection under Article 55(2) was well founded and dismissal of the case was correct in its result. The Yargıtay upheld that decision.

**Yargıtay 6. Hukuk Dairesi, E. 2025/1454, K. 2025/4129, T. 02.12.2025**

In the enforcement of a foreign judgment arising from a construction contract, the respondent argued that his right to be heard was breached because he was not allowed a rejoinder. The regional appeal court rejected this: under Article 55 the case is decided in the simple procedure, where reply and rejoinder are excluded (Article 317(3) HMK). The Yargıtay upheld the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a Turkish court decide an enforcement case without a hearing?

Not where the judgment has an opposing party. The application must be served with the hearing date (Article 55(1) MÖHUK), and the Yargıtay has reversed a decision taken on the papers.

### How long do I have to respond?

Two weeks from service; the court may grant a single extension of up to two weeks (Article 317(2) HMK).

### Can I argue that the foreign judgment is wrong?

No. You may only argue that the conditions for enforcement are not met, that the judgment has been satisfied, or that a reason preventing its satisfaction has arisen (Article 55(2) MÖHUK).

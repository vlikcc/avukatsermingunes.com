Article 73 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) defines the general assembly (genel kurul) of an association (dernek). It is the association's highest decision-making organ and is made up of the registered members. The article concerns every member of an association in Türkiye and the boards that prepare general assembly meetings.

## Article 73 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **II. General assembly**
>
> **1. Nature and composition**
>
> Article 73 – The general assembly is the highest decision-making organ of the association; it consists of the members registered with the association.

Original Turkish text:

> **II. Genel kurul**
>
> **1. Niteliği ve oluşumu**
>
> Madde 73- Genel kurul, derneğin en yetkili karar organı olup; derneğe kayıtlı üyelerden oluşur.

As of November 2026, Article 73 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The reasoning says the article corresponds to Article 57 of the former Civil Code No. 743 and makes the general assembly, made up of all registered members, the association's highest decision-making organ (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### What "registered member" means in practice

A person admitted as a member is entered in the register kept for that purpose (Article 64(2)). Before the meeting, the board draws up the list of members entitled under the statute to attend (Article 14(1) of the Associations Regulation, Dernekler Yönetmeliği). At the venue, identity documents are checked and members sign next to their name on the list before entering. Anyone not on the list, who does not show identification or does not sign is not admitted and may only follow the meeting from a separate area (Article 15(1) of the Regulation).

A legal person can also be a member (Article 64(1)). Its vote is cast by the chair of its board or by the person it appoints to represent it (Article 6 of the Associations Act No. 5253).

### Limits of the "highest" organ

The general assembly elects the other organs, supervises them and may remove them for just cause; it has the final say on admitting and expelling members and deals with any matter not given to another organ (Article 80). If the statute does not assign a task to any organ, the general assembly decides it.

It remains bound by the law and the statute. A member who was present and did not vote for a decision that breaches the law or the statute can ask the court to annul it within one month of the decision; a member who was absent, within one month of learning of it and in any case within three months of the decision (Article 83(1)).

### Associations with branches

How branches (şube) are represented at the association's general assembly is set out in the statute (Article 4(g) of Act No. 5253). Branch general assemblies must finish their ordinary meetings at least two months before the main general assembly (Article 7 of Act No. 5253). Each branch has its own general assembly (Article 95(1) TMK).

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

Unless the statute says otherwise, the general assembly meets where the association has its seat (Article 78(1)). Each member has one vote and must cast it in person (Article 69(1); Article 15 of the Regulation); there is no proxy voting. Two rules matter for members living in Germany or the Netherlands:

- Members are invited at least fifteen days in advance, for example by notice in a newspaper or on the association's website, in writing, or by a message to the email address or phone number the member has given (Article 14(1) of the Regulation). Giving the association up-to-date contact details makes it more likely that you hear about the meeting.
- If the association chooses to, the general assembly can be held online. A system approved by the Ministry is used, the method is stated in the notice, and members log in with a secure electronic signature or two-factor authentication (Article 15/A of the Regulation).

Decisions taken in writing by all members without meeting are valid but do not replace the ordinary meeting (Article 76). On voting rights, see the post on [Article 68 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-68-turkish-civil-code-member-equality); on the first general assembly, see [Article 62 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-62-tmk-first-general-meeting-association).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the highest organ of an association in Türkiye?

The general assembly (Article 73 TMK). It elects and supervises the other organs and may remove them for just cause (Article 80).

### Can I attend the general assembly online from abroad?

Yes, if the association has decided to hold it online and said so in the notice; you log in with a secure electronic signature or two-factor authentication (Article 15/A of the Associations Regulation).

### Can someone vote for me by proxy?

No. Members must vote in person (Article 69(1) TMK).

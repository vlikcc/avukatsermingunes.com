Article 74 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) governs the ordinary general meeting (olağan genel kurul) of an association (dernek). The meeting is held at the time set in the statute, when the board calls it, and must take place at least once every three years. The article matters to boards and to members who want to know why no meeting has been called.

## Article 74 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **2. Meetings**
>
> **a. Ordinary meeting**
>
> Article 74 – The general assembly meets at the time specified in the statute when called by the board.
>
> (Second paragraph amended: 4/11/2004 – Act No. 5253, Art. 38) Ordinary general meetings must be held at least once every three years.

Original Turkish text:

> **2. Toplanması**
>
> **a. Olağan toplantı**
>
> Madde 74- Genel kurul, tüzükte belirtilen zamanda yönetim kurulunun çağrısı üzerine toplanır.
>
> (Değişik ikinci fıkra: 4/11/2004-5253/38 md.) Olağan genel kurul toplantılarının en geç üç yılda bir yapılması zorunludur.

As of November 2026, the amendment table of the text on mevzuat.gov.tr lists Article 74 only under Act No. 5253 (in force since 23 November 2004); there has been no later change (latest law in the table: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The reasoning says the board makes the call, and the maximum interval is meant to stop the board from misusing its power to call the general assembly. It refers to the original wording and mentions two years; Act No. 5253 extended this to three years (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### The statute sets the timing

How and when the general assembly meets must be stated in the statute (Article 4(1)(d) of the Associations Act No. 5253, Dernekler Kanunu). The statute may choose a cycle of one, two or three years, but not longer. Article 13 of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği) repeats the rule.

The call itself is dealt with elsewhere: members are invited at least fifteen days in advance with the date, time, place and agenda (Article 77 TMK; Article 14 of the Regulation). The venue and quorum are in Article 78, the conduct of the meeting in Article 79. Where an association has branches, the branches must finish their ordinary meetings at least two months before the main general assembly (Article 7 of Act No. 5253).

### If the ordinary meeting is not held

The law attaches three consequences:

- Association officers who fail to call the general assembly on time are given an administrative fine (Article 32(1)(b) of Act No. 5253). The amount applying this year could not be confirmed from an official source, so no figure is given here.
- If the ordinary general meeting fails to take place twice in a row, the association comes to an end automatically. Anyone concerned can ask the civil court of peace (sulh hâkimi) to declare that it has ended (Article 87(1)(5) and last paragraph TMK).
- Decisions taken by all members in writing, or at a meeting held without a formal call, do not replace the ordinary meeting (Article 76(2) TMK).

If the board does not call the ordinary meeting, members can ask for an extraordinary one: one fifth of the members can request it in writing, and if the board still does not call it, a single member can ask the civil court of peace to appoint three members to call the meeting (Article 75). The details are in the post on [Article 75 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-75-tmk-extraordinary-general-meeting-association). On the general assembly as the highest organ, see [Article 73 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-73-tmk-association-general-assembly).

## Frequently asked questions

### How often must an association in Türkiye hold a general meeting?

At the time set in its statute, and at least once every three years (Article 74(2) TMK).

### What happens if the ordinary general meeting is not held?

The officers face an administrative fine (Article 32(1)(b) of Act No. 5253). If it is missed twice in a row, the association ends automatically (Article 87(1)(5) TMK).

If you do not pay the aidat for your apartment in Turkey, meaning your share of the common expenses and advance payments, the building manager or any other unit owner can start enforcement proceedings or sue you. Late compensation (gecikme tazminatı) runs at five percent per month for every day of delay (Article 20 of the Turkish Condominium Law No. 634, Kat Mülkiyeti Kanunu, KMK). If a tenant lives in the apartment, the tenant is liable together with you, but only up to the rent the tenant owes (Article 22 KMK).

This article is for unit owners (kat maliki) who live in Germany, the Netherlands or elsewhere in Europe and own an apartment in Turkey, and for building managements. It explains the rules and what to watch for when the debt is pursued while you live abroad.

As of November 2026, the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr shows that the KMK was last amended by Law No. 7579 (Official Gazette of 22 May 2026, No. 33261, in force on publication). That law changed Articles 35, 37 and 70. It did not touch Articles 20 and 22, which set out what happens when the contributions are not paid.

## How much aidat, and who pays? Article 20 KMK

The law does not use the word "aidat". It speaks of the "expense and advance share" (gider ve avans payı). Unless the owners have agreed otherwise, each owner pays (Article 20(1) KMK):

- the costs of the caretaker, heating operator, gardener and night watchman, and the advance collected for them, **in equal shares**;
- insurance premiums, the maintenance, protection, strengthening and repair of all common areas, the manager's fee and the operating costs of shared facilities, and the advance collected for them, **in proportion to the owner's land share** (arsa payı).

You cannot avoid paying by waiving your use of common areas or by arguing that your unit does not need them (Article 20(1)(c)). An apartment that stands empty for most of the year owes the same.

The amount of the advance comes from the annual operating budget (işletme projesi). Law No. 7579 changed this in May 2026. The budget is now approved by the general meeting of unit owners (kat malikleri kurulu). If there is no approved budget, the manager prepares a provisional one, which the meeting must approve within three months at the latest (Article 37(1)). The manager collects the advance until the budget is approved (Article 35(1)(d)). Where a budget already exists, the amount in the provisional budget may not rise by more than the revaluation rate announced under repeated Article 298 of the Tax Procedure Law (last paragraph of Article 37). You can check the yearly rate in the announcement of the Turkish Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi).

## Late compensation on aidat: five percent per month

The law states the consequence of late payment directly. Unofficial translation:

> A unit owner who does not pay the full expense and advance share is obliged to pay late compensation calculated at five percent per month for the days of delay in payment.

Original Turkish text:

> Gider ve avans payının tamamını ödemeyen kat maliki ödemede geciktiği günler için aylık yüzde beş hesabıyla gecikme tazminatı ödemekle yükümlüdür.

Law No. 5711 reduced the rate from ten to five percent, and it has stayed the same since 28 November 2007. It is calculated on the unpaid amount according to the number of days late.

People often ask whether statutory interest can be claimed on top of the late compensation. Article 20 KMK only deals with late compensation, and no Court of Cassation (Yargıtay) decision on the point was checked for this article. Look at your building's management plan (yönetim planı) as well, because Article 20 applies "unless the owners have agreed otherwise".

## Tenant not paying aidat: who is liable?

Tenants, holders of a right of residence and anyone else who uses the unit on a lasting basis are jointly and severally liable with the owner for the expense and advance share and the late compensation (Article 22(1), see also Article 18(2) KMK). The law protects the tenant. Unofficial translation:

> However, the tenant's liability is limited to the amount of rent the tenant is obliged to pay, and any payment the tenant makes is deducted from the rent owed.

So the manager can ask the tenant for the aidat directly and can also pursue the tenant. Whatever the tenant pays to the management for this reason comes off the rent owed to you. If your lease makes the tenant responsible for the aidat and the tenant does not pay, that is a matter under the lease; warning notices and eviction for unpaid ancillary costs are covered in [tenant not paying rent in Turkey](/en/publications/tenant-not-paying-rent-turkey-eviction). Towards the management, you as owner remain the debtor.

## How the management collects: enforcement, lawsuit and mediation

Any other unit owner or the manager can sue the owner who does not pay, or start enforcement proceedings, under the management plan, the KMK and the general rules (Article 20(2) KMK). Taking these steps is one of the manager's statutory duties (Article 35(1)(i)).

Most managements start with enforcement without a judgment (ilamsız icra takibi) at the enforcement office. The debtor may object within seven days of being served with the payment order, and a timely objection stops the proceedings (Articles 62 and 66 of the Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law No. 2004, İİK). Article 37 KMK matters here: final operating budgets and decisions of the general meeting on operating costs count as documents under Article 68(1) İİK. The management can therefore ask the enforcement court to set the objection aside (itirazın kaldırılması) within six months of being served with it. If it misses that period, it cannot start a new enforcement without judgment for the same claim (Article 68(1) İİK).

The management can also sue in the ordinary courts. All disputes arising from the application of the KMK go to the civil court of peace (sulh hukuk mahkemesi) (Additional Article 1 KMK). Since 1 September 2023, mediation (arabuluculuk) is a precondition for any lawsuit in disputes arising from the KMK (Article 18/B(1)(c) of the Mediation Law No. 6325, HUAK); a lawsuit filed without it is dismissed on procedural grounds (Article 18/A(2)). The law requires mediation "before a lawsuit is filed", so it is not needed to start enforcement proceedings. The HUAK does not say whether an application to the enforcement court to set aside the objection is covered, and no Court of Cassation decision on that point was checked for this article.

## Statutory mortgage and, as a last resort, a transfer claim

If the owner's debt cannot be collected from the tenant or other users either, a statutory mortgage (kanuni ipotek) is registered on the debtor's unit for the amount established by the court, in favour of the other owners, on the written request of the manager or, if there is none, of any owner (Article 22(2) KMK). The last paragraph of Article 893 of the Turkish Civil Code (TMK), which Article 22 applies, makes any advance waiver of this mortgage by the creditors invalid. The owners' claims against the defaulting owner and the other liable persons have priority (Article 22(3)).

The more serious step is the transfer claim under Article 25 KMK. If an owner has caused enforcement or court proceedings **three times within two calendar years** because of unpaid common expenses and advances, the situation is deemed intolerable for the other owners (Article 25(3)(a)). They can then ask the court to transfer ownership of that unit to them in proportion to their land shares, against payment of its value. Unless otherwise agreed, the lawsuit requires a decision of the other owners by a majority of both number and land shares. The right to sue lapses six months after the decision to sue becomes known and in any case five years after the right arose (Article 25(2) and (4)).

## Practical points for owners living abroad

The KMK has no special rule for owners who live abroad. The debt, the late compensation and the enforcement routes are the same. The problems usually lie in notices not reaching you:

- The invitation to the general meeting is either signed by all owners or sent by registered letter (Article 29(2) KMK). The operating budget is likewise notified to the owners or to the people actually using the units, against signature or by registered letter (Article 37(3)). The law does not say to which address.
- If you did not attend the meeting, you can challenge a decision before the civil court of peace where the building is located within one month of learning of it and in any case within six months of the decision date; no time limit applies to decisions that are void (Article 33(1)). Living abroad does not extend the six-month limit.
- The payment order and court papers are served under the Turkish Notification Law. If you have no current address in Turkey, they can be sent abroad; how this works is explained in [service of process from Turkey](/en/publications/hague-service-convention-turkey-germany). The period for objecting runs from service.

The author suggests leaving the manager an address and contact details, asking for the operating budget and meeting decisions every year, and paying the aidat by standing order. If you have given a power of attorney to a relative or a lawyer in Turkey, that person can follow the correspondence and any enforcement. If the apartment is let, a clear clause in the lease on who pays the aidat helps avoid later disputes over the deduction under Article 22 KMK.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who owes the aidat, the owner or the tenant?

Towards the management, the owner (Article 20 KMK). The tenant is jointly liable, but only up to the rent; what the tenant pays to the management is deducted from the rent (Article 22(1)). The lease decides how landlord and tenant share the cost between themselves.

### How much is the late compensation on aidat?

Five percent per month for the days of delay (Article 20(2) KMK), unchanged since 28 November 2007.

### Can I lose my apartment over unpaid aidat?

A statutory mortgage can be registered for a debt established by the court (Article 22(2) KMK). An owner who causes enforcement or court proceedings three times within two calendar years can face a claim to transfer the unit against payment of its value (Article 25).

### Is mediation required for aidat debts?

Before a lawsuit arising from the KMK, yes (Article 18/B(1)(c) HUAK). The law does not require mediation before starting enforcement at the enforcement office.

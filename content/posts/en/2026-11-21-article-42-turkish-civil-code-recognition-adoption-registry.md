Article 42 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) provides that changes in a person's civil status after birth are entered in the birth register. It gives as examples the recognition of a child born outside marriage (tanıma), a court ruling on paternity, correction of parentage, adoption, and the parentage of a foundling becoming known. How these are entered is governed not by the Civil Code but by the Population Services Law No. 5490 (Nüfus Hizmetleri Kanunu), which also has special rules for recognitions and adoptions made abroad.

## Article 42 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **II. Changes in the birth register**
>
> Article 42 – Changes in civil status, in particular the recognition of a child born outside marriage or a judge's ruling on paternity, the correction of parentage, adoption, or the parentage of a found child becoming known, are entered in the register in accordance with the relevant laws.

Original Turkish text:

> **II. Doğum kütüğünde değişiklikler**
>
> Madde 42- Kişisel durumdaki değişiklikler, özellikle evlilik dışı bir çocuğun tanınması veya hâkimin babalığa karar vermesi, soybağının düzeltilmesi, evlât edinme ya da bulunmuş bir çocuğun soybağının belli olması, ilgili kanun hükümlerine göre kütüğe işlenir.

As of November 2026, Article 42 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31/10/2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article corresponds to Article 40 of the former Civil Code No. 743. Because changes to the birth register were dealt with separately in the Population Law No. 1587, the article refers to the relevant laws (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly). Today Law No. 5490 applies.

## Explanation

The words "in particular" show that the list gives examples only. What the cases share is that the entry was not wrong from the start; the person's status changed later. That is why they are distinct from a correction under TMK Article 39.

The rules in Law No. 5490:

- **Recognition:** Where the father recognises the child by a written application to the court, the court reports it; where he does so by a deed before a notary, the notary reports it; in both cases to the civil registry office within ten days of the recognition (Article 28(1)). Recognition by will is reported by the judge who opens the will (Article 28(2)). A recognition declared before the registrar is entered directly (Article 28(3)).
- **Paternity judgment:** A child recognised or whose parentage is established by a paternity judgment is moved to the father's household record with the father's name and surname (Article 28(4)).
- **Adoption:** The court reports the adoption order to the local registry office within ten days; the adoptee's record is moved to the adopter's family register (Article 29(1)).
- **General rule:** Court registries report all decisions requiring an entry to the registry office within ten days after they become final (Article 55(1)).

Correction of parentage (for example after a successful challenge to the husband's paternity) and the parentage of a foundling becoming known are likewise entered on the basis of a court decision or a recognition. For the conditions see [paternity suit in Turkey](/en/publications/paternity-suit-turkey-time-limit-court) and [adoption in Turkey](/en/publications/adoption-in-turkey-requirements-process).

## Practical points for Turkish citizens living abroad

- **Recognition abroad:** Notifications of a recognition made abroad can be filed with the Turkish mission, or, translated into Turkish and certified, with a registry office in Türkiye (Law No. 5490, Article 28(5)).
- **Adoption abroad:** An adoption made before foreign authorities is valid in Türkiye if it complies with Turkish law as to its essential conditions (Article 30(1)). However, for final foreign judicial or administrative adoption decisions to be given effect in Türkiye, a Turkish court must issue a recognition or enforcement decision (tanıma, tenfiz) (Article 30(2)).
- **Foreign judgments:** Paternity, disavowal or adoption decisions given abroad are, as a rule, entered in the Turkish register only after recognition in Türkiye; an administrative act in Türkiye based on a foreign judgment also follows the recognition procedure (MÖHUK Article 58(3)).

In the author's assessment, how Article 30(1) and 30(2) work together, that is, when a foreign adoption can be entered directly and when recognition is needed first, may depend on the specific document and the practice of the mission; this should be checked with the mission before applying.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does the Turkish record change after a paternity judgment?

Once the judgment is final, the court reports it to the registry office (Law No. 5490, Article 55(1)) and the child is moved to the father's household record with his name and surname (Article 28(4)).

### Is a recognition of paternity made abroad entered in the Turkish register?

The notification of a recognition made abroad can be filed with the Turkish mission or, with a certified Turkish translation, with a registry office in Türkiye (Law No. 5490, Article 28(5)).

### Is an adoption made abroad valid in Turkey?

Yes, if it complies with Turkish law as to its essential conditions (Article 30(1)); to give effect to foreign decisions, recognition or enforcement is required (Article 30(2)).

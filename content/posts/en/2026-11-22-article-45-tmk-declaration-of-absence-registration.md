Article 45 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) governs how a court's declaration of absence (gaiplik kararı) reaches the civil register: the judge notifies it and it is entered in the death register. Heirs or the spouse do not have to apply to the registry office themselves. As of November 2026 the court must send the notice within ten days (Population Services Law No. 5490, Article 34(1)).

## Article 45 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **III. Declaration of absence**
>
> Article 45 – A declaration of absence is entered in the death register upon notification by the judge.

Original Turkish text:

> **III. Gaiplik kararı**
>
> Madde 45- Gaiplik kararı, hâkimin bildirmesi üzerine, ölüm kütüğüne kaydolunur.

As of November 2026, Article 45 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31/10/2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article corresponds to Article 43 of the former Civil Code No. 743, with no change in substance (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly).

## Explanation

A court declares a person absent if they disappeared in mortal danger or have not been heard of for a long time and their death is highly probable (Article 32 TMK). Rights that depend on the death can then be exercised as if the death had been proved, with effect from the day of the danger or the last news (Article 35 TMK). The application, public notice and effects are covered under [Article 32](/en/publications/legislation/article-32-tmk-declaration-of-absence-turkey) and [Article 35 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-35-tmk-declaration-of-absence-effects).

Article 45 deals with the register. The details are in Law No. 5490 (Nüfus Hizmetleri Kanunu):

- Courts notify declarations of absence to the local registry office (nüfus müdürlüğü) within ten days, and registry officers enter them in the family register (Article 34(1)).
- Absence is one of the grounds for closing a person's record; no further entries can be made on a closed record. If the ground ceases to exist, the record is reopened (Article 14).

Article 40(2) of the Implementing Regulation repeats that the court's declaration is entered in the person's record and that absence has the legal effects of death.

The entry does not end the marriage by itself. The spouse of a person declared absent cannot remarry unless a court has dissolved the marriage (evliliğin feshi) (Article 131(1) TMK; Regulation Article 40(3)). So the spouse may still appear as married in the register after the entry. If the absent person reappears and the declaration is set aside, the change is written in the remarks column (Article 46 TMK).

## Practical points for people living abroad

When a Turkish court issues the declaration, the court sends the notice; you can check that it was entered with a civil registry extract from e-Devlet. A declaration made by a foreign court, such as a declaration of death under the German Missing Persons Act (Verschollenheitsgesetz), is different. Under Article 58 MÖHUK (the Turkish private international law act), a foreign judgment has res judicata or evidential effect only once it has been recognised; this also applies to non-contentious decisions (paragraph 2) and to administrative acts in Türkiye based on a foreign judgment (paragraph 3). Administrative recognition by the registry office (Law No. 5490, Article 27/A) covers only divorce, annulment, voidness and decisions on whether a marriage exists. In the author's assessment, a foreign declaration of death or absence must therefore first be recognised by a Turkish court before it can be entered. Conditions for recognition: [Article 58 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-58-mohuk-recognition-foreign-judgments-turkey).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who notifies the registry office of a declaration of absence?

The court that issued it, within ten days (Article 45 TMK, Law No. 5490, Article 34(1)).

### Can the spouse remarry once the declaration is registered?

No, only after a court has dissolved the marriage (Article 131(1) TMK).

### Is a German declaration of death valid in Türkiye?

For an entry in the Turkish register it must be recognised by a Turkish court (Article 58 MÖHUK).

Article 60 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) governs the review that follows the founding notice of an association (dernek): the chief local administrative officer (en büyük mülkî amir) examines the notice, the documents and the statute on file within sixty days, and gives the founders thirty days to fix any defect. It matters to every founder, including those who live in Germany or the Netherlands and have set up an association in Türkiye.

## Article 60 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **2. Review**
>
> Article 60 – The accuracy of the founding notice and the documents, and the statute of the association, are reviewed on file by the chief local administrative officer within sixty days.
>
> If the founding notice, the statute or the legal position of the founders is found to be unlawful or incomplete, the founders are immediately asked to remedy or complete it. If the defect is not completed and the unlawfulness not remedied within thirty days of service of this request, the chief local administrative officer informs the public prosecutor's office so that it can bring an action for the dissolution of the association before the competent civil court of first instance (asliye hukuk mahkemesi). The public prosecutor may also ask the court to order that the association's activities be suspended.
>
> If the founding notice, the statute and the documents contain no unlawfulness or defect, or if it has been remedied within the set time, the association is informed of this in writing without delay and is entered in the register of associations.

Original Turkish text:

> **2. İnceleme**
>
> Madde 60- Kuruluş bildirimi ve belgelerin doğruluğu ile dernek tüzüğü, en büyük mülkî amir tarafından altmış gün içinde dosya üzerinden incelenir.
>
> Kuruluş bildiriminde, tüzükte ve kurucuların hukukî durumlarında kanuna aykırılık veya noksanlık tespit edildiği takdirde bunların giderilmesi veya tamamlanması derhâl kuruculardan istenir. Bu istemin tebliğinden başlayarak otuz gün içinde belirtilen noksanlık tamamlanmaz ve kanuna aykırılık giderilmezse; en büyük mülkî amir, yetkili asliye hukuk mahkemesinde derneğin feshi konusunda dava açması için durumu Cumhuriyet savcılığına bildirir. Cumhuriyet savcısı mahkemeden derneğin faaliyetinin durdurulmasına karar verilmesini de isteyebilir.
>
> Kuruluş bildiriminde, tüzükte ve belgelerde kanuna aykırılık veya noksanlık bulunmaz ya da bu aykırılık veya noksanlık belirli sürede giderilmiş bulunursa; keyfiyet derhâl derneğe yazıyla bildirilir ve dernek, dernekler kütüğüne kaydedilir.

As of November 2026, Article 60 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The official reasoning says the article sets out in detail how the founding application is reviewed. Paragraph 1 gives the chief administrative officer of the place of domicile the task of checking, within sixty days at most, whether the notice, its annexes and the statute are accurate and whether anything is missing or unlawful; paragraph 2 explains what the officer must do if problems are found (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### When the clock starts and who reviews

The association already has legal personality from the moment the founding notice is filed (Article 59 TMK). The review comes afterwards. Under Article 6(1) of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği), the sixty days run from the date of the receipt issued when the notice is filed. The officer reviews the file in person or through the authorised civil society relations unit; for associations founded in a district (ilçe), the provincial governor's office (valilik) also reviews the statute. The district unit forwards the notice and statute to the provincial directorate within seven days (Regulation, Article 5). Keep the receipt: it proves when legal personality and the review period began.

The law does not say what happens if the sixty days pass; no consequence is attached to the deadline.

### What is checked

The review covers the founding notice, the statute and the legal position of the founders. The statute must contain the mandatory items listed in Article 58 TMK and Article 4 of the Associations Act No. 5253, such as how the general meeting is convened, how the board and the audit committee are elected and how assets are wound up on dissolution. Words such as "Türk", "Türkiye", "Milli" or "Cumhuriyet" may appear in an association's name only with permission from the Ministry of the Interior (Article 28, Act No. 5253). On the founders' capacity and restrictions, see the article on [Article 57 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-57-turkish-civil-code-right-to-form-association).

### The defect letter and thirty days

The thirty days run from service of the officer's request on the founders. If the defect remains, the officer cannot close the association; the officer only reports the matter to the public prosecutor. The prosecutor brings the dissolution action before the civil court of first instance and may ask for activities to be suspended (Article 60(2) TMK; Regulation, Article 6(3)).

A different rule applies later on. Errors in an association's ongoing business that are not criminal must be corrected within thirty days of a written request by the administrative officer, and failure to do so carries an administrative fine (Articles 17 and 32(ı), Act No. 5253). For defects at the founding stage, the consequence is not a fine but the route to a dissolution action.

### Positive outcome

If nothing is wrong, or the defect was fixed in time, the authority informs the chair of the provisional board in writing (Regulation, Article 6(2)). That letter starts the six-month period for the first general meeting (Article 62 TMK).

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

If some founders live in Germany or the Netherlands, it is practical to follow correspondence through the association's address in Türkiye and the provisional board, so that the thirty days from service do not run out unnoticed. The association's domicile is in Türkiye and the notice is filed with the authority there; how that domicile is determined is explained in the article on [Article 51 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-51-turkish-civil-code-domicile-legal-person). If foreign nationals are among the founders, copies of documents showing their right to settle in Türkiye are attached to the notice (Regulation, Article 5(e)); if such a document is missing, it will be requested during the review.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does the review of a new association take in Türkiye?

The authority reviews the notice, documents and statute on file within sixty days (Article 60(1) TMK). The period runs from the date of the receipt (Associations Regulation, Article 6(1)).

### Is the association closed if defects are not fixed?

Not automatically. After thirty days the authority reports the matter to the public prosecutor; the civil court of first instance decides on dissolution (Article 60(2) TMK).

### Can the association operate during the review?

It has legal personality from the filing of the notice (Article 59 TMK). Article 60 TMK gives the authority no power to suspend activities; if the defect remains, the public prosecutor asks the court for suspension (Article 60(2) TMK).

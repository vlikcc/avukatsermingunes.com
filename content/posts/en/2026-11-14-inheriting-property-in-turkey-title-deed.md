Property in Turkey passes to the heirs when the deceased dies. The transfer of the title deed (tapu intikali) only records that ownership in the land register; you apply for it at the land registry office (tapu müdürlüğü) with a Turkish certificate of inheritance (mirasçılık belgesi). If you live abroad, you do not have to travel: a representative can apply with a power of attorney made at a Turkish consulate. Separately, you have to file a Turkish inheritance tax return (veraset ve intikal vergisi beyannamesi) within four, six or eight months, depending on where the death occurred and where you are.

This article reflects the law as of November 2026. How to obtain the certificate of inheritance from abroad is covered in [certificate of inheritance in Turkey](/en/publications/certificate-of-inheritance-turkey).

## Who owns the house before the title deed is transferred?

The heirs acquire the estate as a whole by operation of law on the death of the deceased (Art. 599(1) of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721, TMK). Ownership of land is normally acquired by registration, but inheritance is an exception: ownership passes before registration. The same paragraph sets a limit. The heirs can only dispose of the property once their ownership is registered in the land register (Art. 705(2) TMK).

So your father’s flat is already yours, even though the title deed still shows his name. You cannot sell it, give it away or mortgage it until the transfer is registered.

Where there are several heirs, a community of heirs exists until the estate is divided. The heirs own the estate jointly (elbirliği mülkiyeti) and dispose of its rights together (Art. 640(1)–(2) TMK). In joint ownership there are no defined shares; each owner’s right extends to the whole property (Art. 701(2) TMK).

## How is the transfer done, and do all heirs need to take part?

A person who became entitled before registration may apply for registration by submitting the necessary documents (Art. 17(1) of the Land Registry Regulation, Tapu Sicili Tüzüğü). For statutory or appointed heirs, the document required is a certificate of inheritance issued by a competent authority (Art. 20(1)(a) Regulation). The transfer is not a contract, so no official deed (resmî senet) is drawn up; an application form (istem belgesi) is used instead (Art. 22(1) Regulation).

Whether all heirs must take part depends on how the property is to be registered. According to the undated information sheet of the land registry office at the Turkish Consulate General in Berlin:

- **Registration as joint ownership** can be made at the request of a single heir.
- **Registration as co-ownership in shares (paylı mülkiyet),** with each heir’s share shown, requires all heirs to apply in person or through a representative.

This is administrative practice. The underlying rules of the General Directorate of Land Registry and Cadastre were not reviewed for this article. That the practice fits Art. 17(1) of the Regulation and Art. 640(4) TMK (each heir may ask for the estate’s rights to be protected) is the author’s assessment.

To convert joint ownership into co-ownership in shares, the heirs may conclude a written partition agreement (Art. 676(2)–(3) TMK). For registration, the land registry requires either a notarial agreement that authorises registration or an application by all heirs (Art. 20(1)(c) Regulation). One heir can also ask the civil court of peace (sulh mahkemesi) for the conversion (Art. 644 TMK).

What if nobody registers the transfer? The Civil Code, the Land Registry Law and the Regulation set no deadline for the heirs. But if the transfer has not been registered within two years of the death, the land registry office may itself apply to court for a certificate of inheritance and update the register as joint ownership of the heirs. These applications are free of all costs, taxes and fees (Additional Art. 1 of the Land Registry Law No. 2644, Tapu Kanunu).

**Foreign certificate of inheritance.** Under Art. 37 of the Land Registry Law, transfers for foreign individuals are made on the basis of a certificate issued by a Turkish court, or a foreign certificate confirmed by a Turkish court. The Berlin information sheet requires this confirmation for any certificate issued by a foreign court, so a German Erbschein will not be accepted without it.

Heirs who have neither Turkish citizenship nor a Blue Card (Mavi Kart) are subject to the limits in Art. 35 of the Land Registry Law. Property inherited beyond those limits is sold and the proceeds paid out if the owner does not dispose of it within the period set (Art. 35, last paragraph).

## Is a title deed fee payable on the transfer?

The current text of tariff (4) of the Fees Law No. 492 (Harçlar Kanunu) on UYAP Mevzuat contains no separate fee item for registering inherited property in the heirs’ names. Tariff item I/3-b expressly excludes inheritance transfers (“intikalinde alınmamak kaydıyla”) from the fee it sets for gifts reverting to the donor and for executing legacies. The Berlin information sheet also lists only the land registry service charge (döner sermaye hizmet bedeli) for the transfer.

Concluding from this that no title deed fee is charged on the transfer is the author’s reading; confirm it with the land registry office beforehand. Ask them for the amount of the service charge as well, which is set each year. A later partition or sale is subject to its own fees (tariff I/3-a and I/20-a).

## Transferring the title deed from abroad: power of attorney

Powers of attorney for land registry transactions must be drawn up by a notary in full form (düzenleme şeklinde); a certified signature is not enough (Art. 89 of the Notaries Law No. 1512, Noterlik Kanunu). Abroad, Turkish consulates carry out these notarial acts (Art. 191, 193). The land registry office asks for a power of attorney made under the Notaries Law that authorises the transaction applied for and bears the signature of the person granting it (Art. 18(4) Regulation).

Have the province, district, block and parcel of the property written into the power of attorney, together with the authority to register the transfer and, if you want it, to convert to co-ownership in shares. If the representative is also to file the tax return, the author’s view is that this should be stated expressly.

The Berlin information sheet also lists the inheritance transfer among the services of the land registry office there. How to apply to it is described in [selling property in Turkey from abroad](/en/publications/selling-property-in-turkey-from-abroad). The sheet is undated, so check the current procedure with the office before applying.

## Turkish inheritance tax: deadlines for heirs living abroad

The transfer of property located in Turkey by inheritance is subject to Turkish inheritance tax, whatever the heirs’ nationality (Art. 1(1) of the Inheritance and Gift Tax Law No. 7338, VİVK). The taxpayer is the heir who acquires the property (Art. 5), and the heir must declare it in a tax return (Art. 7(1)).

The deadlines run from the day after the death (Art. 9(1)):

- Death in Turkey, heir in Turkey: **four months**.
- Death in Turkey, heir abroad: **six months**.
- Death abroad, heir in Turkey: **six months**.
- Death abroad, heir in the same country as the deceased (for example, both in Germany): **four months**.
- Death abroad, heir in a different foreign country (for example, death in Germany, heir in the Netherlands): **eight months**.

Living abroad does not automatically give you more time: if your mother died in Germany and you also live there, you have four months. Where heirs live in different countries, the wording of the law suggests a separate deadline for each heir; this is the author’s assessment.

Taxpayers abroad file their returns with a Turkish consulate (Art. 8(2)). A return may be filed for each taxpayer separately or jointly (Art. 8(3)). The tax is assessed by the tax office where the deceased lived; if the deceased lived abroad, by the tax office of their last residence in Turkey (Art. 6).

## 2026 exemptions, tax rates and payment

Real property is declared at its property tax value (emlak vergisi değeri) (Art. 10(b)). The valuation date is the date of death (Art. 11).

Under General Communiqué (Genel Tebliğ) No. 57 on the Inheritance and Gift Tax Law (Official Gazette of 31 December 2025, No. 33124, 5th repeat issue), from 1 January 2026:

- **TRY 2,907,136** of the inherited share of each descendant, including adopted children, and of the spouse is exempt.
- If there are no descendants, **TRY 5,817,845** of the spouse’s share is exempt.

The remainder is taxed on a progressive scale. For inheritances in 2026: 1% on the first TRY 3,000,000, 3% on the next TRY 7,000,000, 5% on the next TRY 15,000,000, 7% on the next TRY 30,000,000 and 10% on the part above TRY 55,000,000 (Art. 16 VİVK, Art. 4 of the Communiqué). The Communiqué applies these amounts to transfers occurring from 1 January 2026. Because the estate passes on death (Art. 599(1) TMK), the 2026 figures do not apply where the death occurred in 2025.

The assessed tax is paid over three years in two equal instalments each year, in May and November (Art. 19(1)). The same inheritance may also be taxed in Germany or the Netherlands. That is governed by the law of that country and is outside the scope of this article.

## Tax clearance: no bar to registration, but a bar to sale

Under Art. 19(2) VİVK, inherited property is registered without waiting for the tax assessment; the result is reported to the tax office within 15 days of registration. A return not yet filed therefore does not hold up the transfer.

A sale is a different matter. Until the tax attributable to the property has been paid in full, it cannot be transferred or encumbered with a right in rem. Land registry officials may not register a transfer without a tax clearance certificate (ilişik kesme belgesi) from the tax office (Art. 19(2)). If security of the kind listed in Art. 10 of Law No. 6183 is provided for the tax, the transfer may be allowed (Art. 19(3)). If you plan to sell, plan the return and payment accordingly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do all heirs need to be present for the title deed transfer?

According to the Berlin land registry office’s information sheet, registration as joint ownership can be made at the request of one heir; registration in shares requires all heirs, in person or through a representative. A certificate of inheritance is needed in every case (Art. 20(1)(a) Regulation).

### What happens if the inherited property is never transferred?

Ownership still belongs to the heirs (Art. 705(2) TMK), but without registration they cannot sell or mortgage. After two years the land registry office may itself apply for a certificate of inheritance (Additional Art. 1 Land Registry Law). The inheritance tax deadline runs regardless of the transfer.

### Is there inheritance tax in Turkey for heirs living abroad?

Yes. Property in Turkey is subject to Turkish inheritance tax whatever the heir’s nationality (Art. 1(1) VİVK). From 1 January 2026, TRY 2,907,136 per descendant and spouse is exempt, or TRY 5,817,845 for a spouse where there are no descendants.

### Where do heirs abroad file the inheritance tax return?

Under the law, with a Turkish consulate (Art. 8(2) VİVK). The deadline is four months if the deceased and the heir were in the same foreign country, six months if the death occurred in Turkey, and eight months if they were in different foreign countries (Art. 9(1)).

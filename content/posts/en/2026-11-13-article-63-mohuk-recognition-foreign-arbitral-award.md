Article 63 of Turkey’s Law No. 5718 on Private International Law and International Civil Procedure (MÖHUK) governs the recognition (tanıma) of foreign arbitral awards. It contains a single rule: recognition is subject to the provisions on enforcement (tenfiz). It matters to anyone who wants to rely on an award made abroad in Turkish proceedings without having to enforce it.

## Article 63 MÖHUK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **Recognition of foreign arbitral awards**
>
> Article 63 – (1) The recognition of foreign arbitral awards is also subject to the provisions on their enforcement.

Turkish original:

> **Yabancı hakem kararlarının tanınması**
>
> MADDE 63 – (1) Yabancı hakem kararlarının tanınması da tenfizine ilişkin hükümlere tâbidir.

The article has not changed since the law entered into force on 12 December 2007. As of November 2026, the amendment list of the current text in UYAP Mevzuat shows only Article 27 (amended by Law No. 7550).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the official reasoning, the former Law No. 2675 had no provision on the recognition of foreign arbitral awards. Article 63 was added to remove uncertainty in practice by stating that the enforcement provisions also apply to recognition (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, Article 62 of the bill).

## Explanation

Enforcement makes a foreign award enforceable in Turkey. Recognition does not ask for compulsory execution; it asks the court to accept the award as res judicata (kesin hüküm) or as conclusive evidence (kesin delil). If, for example, a dispute already decided by an arbitral tribunal abroad is brought before a Turkish court again, the defendant can rely on recognition of the award.

Article 63 refers to Articles 60 to 62:

- The award must be final and enforceable or binding on the parties; the competent court is determined under Article 60(2). More: [Article 60 MÖHUK – foreign arbitral awards in Turkey](/en/publications/legislation/article-60-mohuk-foreign-arbitral-award-turkey).
- The arbitration agreement, the award and certified translations are filed; Articles 55 to 57 apply by analogy to the procedure (Article 61). More: [Article 61 MÖHUK – enforcement procedure](/en/publications/legislation/article-61-mohuk-enforcement-arbitral-award-turkey).
- The grounds for refusal in Article 62 are also examined on recognition.

On recognition of court judgments (Article 58) and on when res judicata takes effect (Article 59), see [Article 58 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-58-mohuk-recognition-foreign-judgments-turkey) and [Article 59 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-59-mohuk-res-judicata-recognition-date).

Whether recognition of an award must be sought in a separate action or can be examined as a preliminary question in pending proceedings is disputed. The law has no express rule on this for arbitral awards. In the decision below, the Court of Cassation told the court hearing the case to examine an arbitration file under Article 63 read with Articles 58 and 59, but a single decision is not settled case law. How a court will proceed in a particular case is uncertain.

## What this means if you live in Germany or the Netherlands

The article mostly arises in commercial disputes. If you won an arbitration in Germany or the Netherlands and the other side sues on the same matter in Turkey, keep the original or a certified copy of the award, the arbitration clause and information on its finality or binding effect ready; recognition is examined on the basis of these documents.

## Court of Cassation (Yargıtay) case law

**Yargıtay (dissolved) 15th Civil Chamber, E. 2016/5466, K. 2017/1822, 27.04.2017**

In a dispute under a works contract concerning a bank guarantee and the contract price, the defendant stated that it had started arbitration before the International Court of Arbitration against the employer, who was not a party to the case. The Chamber reversed the judgment, among other shortcomings because the court should have examined whether that arbitration had become final under Article 63 read with Articles 58 and 59 MÖHUK and discussed its effect on the case.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the conditions for recognising a foreign arbitral award in Turkey?

The same as for enforcement: the award is final and enforceable or binding (Article 60(1)), the documents listed in Article 61 are filed, and none of the grounds for refusal in Article 62 applies.

### How does recognition differ from enforcement?

Enforcement makes the award enforceable. Recognition only gives it the effect of res judicata or conclusive evidence in Turkey.

### Is reciprocity required?

Not under MÖHUK; Article 61(2) does not refer to Article 54, which contains the reciprocity condition. For awards within the scope of the New York Convention, Turkey’s reservation must also be taken into account.

Article 46 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) is the last provision on the death register and governs how later changes to a death entry are recorded. The old entry is not deleted; the change is written in the remarks column (düşünceler sütunu) of the person's record. It concerns people wrongly registered as dead, relatives of a body identified later, and absent persons who return.

## Article 46 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **IV. Entering changes in the register**
>
> Article 46 – Changes required because a report on which an entry was based is found to be incorrect, because the identity of an unidentified body becomes known, or because a declaration of absence is set aside, are made by writing them in the remarks column of the person's record in the register.

Original Turkish text:

> **IV. Değişikliklerin kütüğe geçirilmesi**
>
> Madde 46- Tescile esas olan bir bildirimin doğru olmadığının tespit edilmesi veya kime ait olduğu bilinmeyen cesedin kimliğinin belli olması ya da gaiplik kararının kaldırılması sebepleriyle zorunlu olan değişiklikler, ilgilinin kütükteki kaydının düşünceler sütununa yazılarak yapılır.

As of November 2026, Article 46 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31/10/2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article corresponds to Article 44 of the former Civil Code No. 743, with no change in substance (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly).

## Explanation

The article names three grounds:

1. The report on which the entry was based turns out to be wrong, for example a death report concerned another person, or the person registered as dead is alive.
2. The identity of an unidentified body becomes known.
3. A declaration of absence is set aside.

Article 46 says how the change is recorded, not who decides on it. That is in the Population Services Law No. 5490 (Nüfus Hizmetleri Kanunu). Without a final court judgment no entry may be corrected and no note may be added that changes its meaning; only clerical errors made when the entry was recorded are corrected by the registry office in line with the source document (Article 35(1)). The Implementing Regulation repeats that where no clerical error is involved, a correction requires a court decision (Article 41(2)). So if the report itself was wrong, a judgment is needed as a rule; an error by the registrar in copying is corrected administratively. On the distinction and procedure: [Article 39 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-39-turkish-civil-code-correcting-civil-registry).

A record closed because of death or absence is reopened when the reason ceases to exist, and events in the meantime are added (Law No. 5490, Article 14(2)). What happens to the property when the absent person returns is governed by Article 585 TMK; see [Article 35 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-35-tmk-declaration-of-absence-effects).

The remarks do not appear on every civil registry extract. Extracts normally show identity data only; other personal data and notes on civil status events are included only if an authorised requester asks for them and states the reason (Regulation Article 50(2)(a)). Extracts requested by a court show all remarks (Article 50(4)).

## Practical points for people living abroad

If you are wrongly shown as dead in the Turkish register, or a relative who died abroad was registered against the wrong person, first establish whether it is a clerical error or a wrong report. If the source document is right but was copied wrongly, apply to the registry office, or to the Turkish mission if it has been given that power (Regulation Article 41(1) and (3)). If the source document itself is wrong, a court decision is needed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is a wrong death entry corrected?

If the error was made when recording the entry, by the registry office; if the report was wrong, by a final court judgment (Law No. 5490, Article 35(1)). The change goes in the remarks column (Article 46 TMK).

### What happens to the record when an absent person returns?

The declaration of absence is set aside, the closed record is reopened and the change is noted in the remarks column (Article 46 TMK, Law No. 5490, Article 14(2)).

Article 26 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) protects a person's right to their name (ad). If your right to bear a name is disputed, you can ask the court to confirm it; if someone else uses your name without right, you can ask for the use to stop and, if they are at fault, also claim compensation for financial loss and, where justified, non-pecuniary damages (manevi tazminat). Alongside the general protection of personality in Articles 24 and 25 TMK, this is a special rule for the name only.

## Article 26 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **III. Right to a name**
>
> **1. Protection of the name**
>
> Article 26 – A person whose use of their name is disputed may bring an action to have their right confirmed.
>
> A person whose name is used by another without right may demand that this use cease; if the person using it without right is at fault, they may also demand compensation for their pecuniary loss and, where the nature of the wrong suffered so requires, payment of non-pecuniary damages.

Turkish original:

> **III. Ad üzerindeki hak**
>
> **1. Adın korunması**
>
> Madde 26- Adının kullanılması çekişmeli olan kişi, hakkının tespitini dava edebilir.
>
> Adı haksız olarak kullanılan kişi buna son verilmesini; haksız kullanan kusurlu ise ayrıca maddî zararının giderilmesini ve uğradığı haksızlığın niteliği gerektiriyorsa manevî tazminat ödenmesini isteyebilir.

The article has not changed since 1 January 2002. As of November 2026, the amendment table of the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr does not list Article 26; the latest law in the table is Law No. 7589 (Articles 440 and 444, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article corresponds to Article 25 of the former Code; the more common word "ad" replaces "isim". The article deals only with protection against others appropriating the name. The reasoning stresses that the action to stop the use requires neither damage nor fault, while damages claims require fault. Following Article 29 of the Swiss Civil Code, non-pecuniary damages here take the form of a sum of money (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly).

## Explanation

### Two different situations

Paragraph 1 protects a person whose name is **disputed**. If someone claims you are not entitled to a particular first name or surname, you can ask for your right to be confirmed. The aim is to remove uncertainty; the other person does not need to be using the name themselves.

Paragraph 2 covers **unauthorised use**. If someone uses your name without right for themselves, or for a product, a business or an online account, you can ask for it to stop. Two people who share a name and each use their own name are not misusing it; the use is wrongful when it is made without right and causes confusion or harms an interest worth protecting.

### When does fault matter?

The action to stop the use requires neither fault nor damage. Compensation for financial loss and non-pecuniary damages are available only if the user is at fault; for non-pecuniary damages, the nature of the wrong must also justify it.

### Which names are protected?

The first name and surname recorded in the civil registry are protected. It is also accepted that a pseudonym or stage name by which a person is known can be protected; this is not spelled out in the Code, and it depends on whether the name actually identifies the person. Changing a name is a separate matter, governed by Article 27 TMK.

## What this means in practice if you live abroad

A fake social media profile in your name, or a business in Türkiye using your name in its advertising without permission, are typical cases under Article 26 TMK. They also fall under the general protection of personality (Articles 24 and 25 TMK); for jurisdiction and the limitation period for damages, see [Article 25 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-25-turkish-civil-code-personality-rights-actions).

If your name is spelled differently in German or Dutch documents (for example "ue" instead of "ü"), that is not wrongful use under Article 26 TMK. It is a mismatch between documents, dealt with by correcting the registry entry or the document. More on that in [How to change your name in Türkiye](/en/publications/how-to-change-your-name-in-turkey).

## Frequently asked questions

### When can I bring an action to protect my name?

When your right to bear your name is disputed (confirmation), or when someone else uses your name without right (stopping the use and, if applicable, damages), Article 26 TMK.

### Is fault required to stop the use of my name?

No. The action to stop the use requires neither fault nor damage. For damages, the user must be at fault.

### Which rule applies to a fake profile using my name?

Article 26 TMK as wrongful use of a name, and Articles 24 and 25 TMK as an infringement of personality rights.

Article 76 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) lets the general assembly (genel kurul) of an association (dernek) take decisions without a meeting or without a formal call in two situations. Both require every member to take part, and neither replaces the ordinary meeting. The article is mainly relevant to small associations.

## Article 76 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **c. Decisions taken without a meeting or without a call**
>
> Article 76 – Decisions taken with the written participation of all members without meeting, and decisions taken by all members of the association meeting together without following the call procedure laid down in the law, are valid.
>
> Taking decisions in this way does not replace the ordinary meeting.

Original Turkish text:

> **c. Toplantısız veya çağrısız alınan kararlar**
>
> Madde 76- Bütün üyelerin bir araya gelmeksizin yazılı katılımıyla alınan kararlar ile dernek üyelerinin tamamının kanunda yazılı çağrı usulüne uymaksızın bir araya gelerek aldığı kararlar geçerlidir.
>
> Bu şekilde karar alınması olağan toplantı yerine geçmez.

As of November 2026, Article 76 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the first paragraph makes valid both the decisions all members take in writing without meeting and the decisions all members take at a meeting held without following the call procedure; the second paragraph provides that decisions cannot be taken in this way in place of the ordinary general meeting (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### Two routes

In a decision without a meeting (toplantısız karar), members do not come together. A proposal is circulated and each member takes part in writing. In a meeting without a call (çağrısız toplantı), members do meet, but the fifteen-day notice under Article 77 and the notice rules in Article 14 of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği) have not been followed.

In both cases the test is "all members". If a single member does not take part or cannot be reached, Article 76 does not apply and the general assembly has to be called under Article 77. The call rules exist so that members learn of the meeting; when everyone takes part, that purpose has already been met.

### Does the decision have to be unanimous?

This point is disputed. The law requires all members to take part, but does not expressly require a unanimous decision. Whether, once everyone has taken part, the absolute majority under Article 81 is enough (two thirds for amending the statute or dissolving the association), or whether all members must agree to both the procedure and the decision, cannot be read clearly from the law. There is no express provision and no binding court decision on the point; it is uncertain which reading a court would adopt in a particular dispute.

### No substitute for the ordinary meeting

The association must hold its ordinary general meeting at the time set in its statute and at least once every three years ([Article 74 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-74-tmk-ordinary-general-meeting-association)). A decision under Article 76 does not satisfy that duty. If the ordinary meeting fails to take place twice in a row, the association ends automatically (Article 87(1)(5) TMK). Since these decisions are still decisions of the general assembly, in the author's view the annulment action under Article 83 is open against them where they breach the law or the statute; the Act has no separate provision on this.

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

If a member living abroad does not take part, the decision cannot rest on Article 76, because "all members" includes that member too. The law does not say in what form written participation must be given (handwritten signature, email, electronic signature). The rule on online general meetings (Article 15/A of the Regulation) covers meetings held electronically, not decisions taken without a meeting. It is therefore safer to take part with a signed letter so that your participation cannot be disputed later; this is a practical suggestion. On voting in person, see the post on [Article 69 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-69-tmk-voting-rights-association-general-assembly).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an association in Türkiye decide without holding a meeting?

Yes, if all members take part in writing (Article 76(1) TMK). If even one member does not, this route is closed.

### Does a decision without a meeting replace the ordinary general meeting?

No (Article 76(2) TMK). The ordinary meeting must be held at the time set in the statute and at least every three years (Article 74).

Article 49 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sets the condition for the capacity to act (fiil ehliyeti) of legal persons: once a legal person has the organs required by law and by its founding documents, it can acquire rights and take on obligations through its own acts. The article matters to founders of associations and foundations, board members, and anyone dealing with a legal person whose organs are incomplete.

## Article 49 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **C. Capacity to act**
>
> **I. Condition**
>
> Article 49 – Legal persons acquire capacity to act once they have the organs required by law and by their founding documents.

Original Turkish text:

> **C. Fiil ehliyeti**
>
> **I. Koşulu**
>
> Madde 49- Tüzel kişiler, kanuna ve kuruluş belgelerine göre gerekli organlara sahip olmakla, fiil ehliyetini kazanırlar.

As of November 2026, Article 49 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31/10/2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article corresponds to Article 47 of the former Civil Code No. 743 without change in substance. Instead of "Medenî hakları kullanmak salâhiyeti" it uses "fiil ehliyeti", the translation of "Handlungsfähigkeit" from the German text of the Swiss Civil Code, a term now settled in Turkish law. The word "nizamname" (statutes) was replaced by "kuruluş belgeleri" (founding documents), because not every founding document of a private-law legal person is a set of statutes. Since a legal person has no natural will, one can speak of capacity to act only once organs exist that form its will and whose acts are attributed to it (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### Which organs are "required"?

Article 49 does not list them; it refers to the law and the founding documents. For an association (dernek), the mandatory organs are the general assembly, the board of directors and the supervisory board (Article 72 TMK), and the statutes must state the duties, powers, method of election and number of members of the board and the supervisory board (Article 4(1)(f) of the Associations Act No. 5253, Dernekler Kanunu). The association is already a legal person, but must hold its first general assembly and form its mandatory organs within six months of the written notice that its documents have been reviewed and it has been entered in the register (Article 60, last paragraph, and Article 62 TMK). If it does not, the association comes to an end automatically (Article 87(1), point 2 TMK). A foundation (vakıf) must have a management organ; the founder may provide for further organs (Article 109 TMK), and the foundation deed sets out its organisation and management (Article 106 TMK).

For natural persons, capacity to act depends on capacity of judgement, majority and not being under guardianship; see the [post on Articles 9 and 10 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-9-10-turkish-civil-code-capacity-to-act). For a legal person, the organ takes their place. The limit of legal capacity is covered in the post on [Article 48 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-48-tmk-legal-capacity-legal-persons), the effects of acts by organs in the post on [Article 50 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-50-tmk-organs-liability-legal-persons).

### When an organ is later missing

Losing an organ does not always end legal personality, but there may be no one left to act for the legal person. If it has become impossible to form an association's board under its statutes, the association ends automatically, and any interested party can ask the civil court of peace (sulh hâkimi) to record this (Article 87 TMK). Beyond that, the law provides several routes:

- If a legal person lacks its required organs and its management cannot be secured otherwise, the guardianship authority appoints a management curator (yönetim kayyımı) (Article 427(4) TMK).
- If members of the organs of a new foundation drop out through death, resignation or any other reason and the foundation deed says nothing, the court elects replacements on the decision of the competent organ and after obtaining the opinion of the General Directorate of Foundations (Article 8 of the Foundations Act No. 5737, Vakıflar Kanunu).
- Persons convicted of the offences listed in Article 3(3) of Law No. 5253 (including terrorist financing, drug trafficking and money laundering) may not sit on any association organ other than the general assembly; if the conviction comes after election, the office ends (paragraph added by Law No. 7262).

Those elected to the organs must be notified to the associations authority within thirty days after the general assembly (Article 23 of Law No. 5253).

## Practical points for people living abroad

If you live in Germany or the Netherlands and want to set up a foundation in Türkiye, there is one limit to know: the management organ of new foundations is formed under the foundation deed, and the majority of its members must be resident in Türkiye (Article 6, last paragraph, of Law No. 5737). If you plan an organ made up only of family members living abroad, the deed has to be drafted differently. Foreign nationals may set up new foundations in Türkiye on the basis of legal and de facto reciprocity (Article 5(4)). How legal personality arises is explained in the [post on Article 47 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-47-turkish-civil-code-legal-personality).

## Frequently asked questions

### When does a legal person acquire capacity to act?

Once the organs required by law and its founding documents have been formed (Article 49 TMK). For an association, this happens when the mandatory organs are elected at the first general assembly (Articles 62 and 72 TMK).

### What happens to an association with no board?

If it has become impossible to form the board under the statutes, the association ends automatically (Article 87(1), point 4 TMK). If the association continues but its management cannot be secured otherwise, the guardianship authority appoints a management curator (Article 427(4) TMK).

### Can all managers of a foundation live abroad?

No. The majority of the members of a new foundation's management organ must be resident in Türkiye (Article 6 of Law No. 5737).

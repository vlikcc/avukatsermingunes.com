Article 65 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) covers the automatic end of membership of an association (dernek). If a member later loses a quality that the law or the association's statute requires for membership, the membership ends without any decision or declaration. This concerns members of associations in Türkiye and the boards that keep the membership register.

## Article 65 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **II. Termination**
>
> **1. Automatically**
>
> Article 65 – The membership of those who subsequently lose the qualities required for membership by law or by the statute ends automatically.

Original Turkish text:

> **II. Sona ermesi**
>
> **1. Kendiliğinden**
>
> Madde 65- Üyelik için kanunda veya tüzükte aranılan nitelikleri sonradan kaybedenlerin dernek üyeliği kendiliğinden sona erer.

As of November 2026, Article 65 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The reasoning explains that termination of membership is dealt with in three articles (“automatically”, “by leaving” and “by expulsion”) and that under this article membership ends automatically when the conditions laid down in the law or the statute are lost (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### Three ways membership ends

Membership ends automatically (Article 65), when the member leaves ([Article 66](/en/publications/legislation/article-66-tmk-leaving-association-turkey)) or when the association expels the member (Article 67). What sets Article 65 apart is that no one's will is involved. The member does not give notice and the association takes no decision.

### Which qualities?

The article names two sources: the law and the statute.

A statutory example is in Article 3 of the Associations Law No. 5253 (Dernekler Kanunu): persons over 18 may not be members of a children's association. When such a member turns 18, a condition set by law falls away.

The statute can also set conditions; Article 4(1)(c) of the Associations Law requires it to state the conditions of joining (on the statute, see [Article 58 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-58-turkish-civil-code-association-statute)). If the statute requires, for example, a particular profession or residence in a particular place, membership ends under Article 65 once that condition is no longer met.

Capacity to act (Article 64(1)) is less clear.

> This point is disputed. Article 65 refers to “qualities required by law”, and Article 64(1) names capacity to act as a condition of the right to join. The Code does not say expressly whether membership ends if a member is later placed under guardianship (kısıtlanma), and no binding court decision on this could be verified for this post. How a court would decide in a given case is uncertain.

### What the association has to do

Because membership ends by itself, the association does not decide anything; it records the end. Under Article 23 of the Associations Law, it reports ended memberships to the associations authority where it has its seat within 45 days of the end. Under Article 92 of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği), this is done on the “Üye Değişiklik Bildirimi” form (Annex 27). Board members who fail to remove from the register persons who have lost the right to be members face an administrative fine under Article 32(a) of the Associations Law; the current amount is not given here.

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

The statute of a hometown association (hemşehri derneği) in Türkiye may make residence in a particular place a condition of membership. Whether moving abroad affects your membership depends on how that condition is worded. The law itself does not require Turkish citizens to live in Türkiye, so check the statute first.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the association have to take a decision when membership ends automatically?

No. Membership ends when the quality is lost (Article 65 TMK); the association records this and reports it within 45 days (Article 23, Associations Law).

### Does my membership end if I move abroad?

The law has no such rule. If the statute makes residence in a particular place a condition, membership can end under Article 65.

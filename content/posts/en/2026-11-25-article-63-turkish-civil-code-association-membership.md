Article 63 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) opens the part of the Code on membership of an association (dernek). It protects freedom on both sides: no one can be made to join, and no association has to admit someone it does not want. This matters to anyone who wants to join or leave an association in Türkiye, including people living in Germany or the Netherlands who belong to a hometown or village association (hemşehri derneği, köy derneği) back in Türkiye.

## Article 63 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **B. Membership**
>
> **I. Acquisition**
>
> **1. Rule**
>
> Article 63 – No one may be compelled to become a member of an association, and no association may be compelled to admit members.

Original Turkish text:

> **B. Üyelik**
>
> **I. Kazanılması**
>
> **1. Kural**
>
> Madde 63- Hiç kimse, bir derneğe üye olmaya ve hiçbir dernek de üye kabul etmeye zorlanamaz.

As of November 2026, Article 63 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The reasoning counts the right of assembly and association among the fundamental rights, refers to Article 33 of the Turkish Constitution and makes membership a matter of the person's own will (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### Link to Article 33 of the Constitution

Under Article 33(1) of the Constitution, everyone has the freedom to form associations without prior permission, to join them and to leave them. Article 33(2) adds that no one may be compelled to become or remain a member of an association. Article 63 TMK repeats the individual's side of this guarantee and adds the association's side, which the Constitution does not mention.

### The right to join and the decision to admit

Every natural person with capacity to act (fiil ehliyeti), and every legal person, has the right to join associations (Article 64(1) TMK). That is a general right, not a claim to be admitted to a particular association. The board decides on a written application within thirty days at most and informs the applicant in writing (Article 64(2)). The general assembly has the final say on admission and expulsion (Article 80(1)). The Code contains no provision giving a rejected applicant a right to be admitted; Article 63 says the opposite.

Once you are a member, the association may not treat members differently on grounds of language, race, colour, sex, religion and sect, family, group or class (Article 68(1)). This equality rule protects members, not applicants.

### No one has to stay

No one can be compelled to remain a member; every member may leave by giving written notice (Article 66). A departing member still owes the dues for the time of membership (Article 70(1)) and has no claim to the association's assets (Article 68(3)). The association must report new members and ended memberships to the associations authority within 45 days (Article 23, Associations Law No. 5253).

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

For Turkish citizens the Code does not require residence in Türkiye; capacity to act is enough (Article 64(1)). People who hold only a foreign nationality need the right to settle in Türkiye (yerleşme hakkı); this does not apply to honorary membership (Article 93).

To leave an association, the only form the Code requires is written notice (Article 66), which you can send from abroad. As of November 2026, the e-Devlet portal also lists a Ministry of the Interior service called "Dernek Üyeliği Sorgulama ve Sonlandırma" (checking and ending association membership). Leaving does not cancel dues owed for the period of membership (Article 70(1)).

For the legal nature and founding of an association, see the posts on [Article 56 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-56-turkish-civil-code-association-definition) and [Article 57 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-57-turkish-civil-code-right-to-form-association).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an association in Türkiye reject my membership application?

Yes. No association can be compelled to admit members (Article 63 TMK). The board decides within thirty days at most and informs you in writing (Article 64(2)).

### How do I leave an association in Türkiye?

By written notice (Article 66 TMK). Dues for the time you were a member remain payable (Article 70(1)).

### Can I join an association in Türkiye while living abroad?

As a Turkish citizen, yes: residence in Türkiye is not required, capacity to act is enough (Article 64(1)). Foreigners need the right to settle in Türkiye (Article 93).

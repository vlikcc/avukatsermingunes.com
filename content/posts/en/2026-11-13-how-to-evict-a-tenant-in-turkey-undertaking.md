One way to evict a tenant in Turkey is the written undertaking to vacate (tahliye taahhüdü): the tenant promises the landlord in writing to leave the property on a set date. If the tenant does not leave, the landlord can end the lease **within one month of that date**, either by starting enforcement proceedings or by filing a lawsuit (Article 352(1) of the Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098, TBK).

The undertaking only works if it is in writing, names a specific move-out date and was given after the property was handed over to the tenant. According to the current texts in UYAP Mevzuat (November 2026), Article 352 TBK and the eviction provisions in Articles 272–276 of the Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law (İcra ve İflas Kanunu, İİK) have not changed in recent years. The most recent amendment to the TBK, Law No. 7589 (in force since 31 July 2026), only touches Article 55. What has changed in tenancy disputes is mandatory mediation, which has its own section below.

## What is an undertaking to vacate and where is it in the law?

For residential and covered commercial premises, the landlord cannot remove a tenant just because the lease term has expired. Unless the tenant gives notice at least fifteen days before the end of a fixed term, the lease is extended by one year (Article 347(1) TBK). The undertaking to vacate is the exception, based on the tenant's own choice. Article 352(1) TBK reads:

Unofficial translation:

> If the tenant, after the leased property has been handed over, has undertaken in writing towards the landlord to vacate the leased property on a specific date and has not vacated it, the landlord may terminate the lease within one month from that date by applying for enforcement or by filing a lawsuit.

Original Turkish text:

> Kiracı, kiralananın teslim edilmesinden sonra, kiraya verene karşı, kiralananı belli bir tarihte boşaltmayı yazılı olarak üstlendiği hâlde boşaltmamışsa kiraya veren, kira sözleşmesini bu tarihten başlayarak bir ay içinde icraya başvurmak veya dava açmak suretiyle sona erdirebilir.

A lease of a flat in Turkey is governed by Turkish law even if you live in Germany, the Netherlands or elsewhere ([Article 25 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-25-mohuk-immovable-property-contracts)). Rent increases are a separate topic: see [rent increase in Turkey](/en/publications/rent-increase-turkey-legal-limit).

## Validity requirements

The 3rd Civil Chamber of the Court of Cassation (Yargıtay) requires the following (3. HD, E. 2026/2877, K. 2026/2766, 05.05.2026; 3. HD, E. 2025/3374, K. 2025/4997, 20.10.2025):

- **Written form.** A spoken promise is not enough.
- **Signed by the tenant** or an authorised representative. If there are several tenants, all of them must sign.
- **A specific move-out date.** "I will leave when the landlord asks" is not a date.
- **Given after handover.** The statute says so expressly. Decision 2026/2877 refers to an undertaking given "a reasonable time after handover".

**Does the tenant's spouse have to sign?** In decision E. 2025/3374, K. 2025/4997, issued to resolve conflicting rulings of the regional courts of appeal, the 3rd Civil Chamber held: a spouse who is not a tenant becomes a party to the lease by notifying the landlord that the flat is the family home (aile konutu) (Article 194 of the Turkish Civil Code, TMK). A valid undertaking that the tenant gave alone before that notice stays valid. After the notice, both spouses must sign, or the other spouse must give express consent.

## Undertakings signed with the lease, or with the date left blank

**Given before handover: invalid.** In 3. HD, E. 2025/2741, K. 2025/5408, 18.11.2025, the lease said the property would not be handed over without an undertaking and that the undertaking was an annex to the lease. The Chamber treated this as proof that the undertaking was given before handover and held it invalid.

**The burden of proof is on the tenant.** The Assembly of Civil Chambers (Hukuk Genel Kurulu), E. 2017/975, K. 2021/1108, 28.09.2021, held that a tenant who claims the undertaking was signed before the lease must prove it. Filling in the issue and move-out dates later does not by itself make the undertaking invalid; the tenant has to prove the dates were filled in contrary to what was agreed. The decision was taken by majority, and the view that the burden should lie on the landlord was also put forward in the Assembly. In E. 2025/1914, K. 2025/5401, 17.11.2025, the 3rd Civil Chamber upheld an eviction because it had not been proven that the undertaking was drawn up on the same day as the lease.

**A new lease signed after the undertaking.** In 3. HD, E. 2026/2877, K. 2026/2766, 05.05.2026, the Chamber held that the undertaking lost its effect because the parties later signed a new lease and so renewed the tenancy. This was a reversal in the interest of the law (kanun yararına bozma), which did not change the outcome between the parties but shows the Chamber's approach.

## Enforcement or lawsuit? Routes and deadlines

Whichever route you choose, the first limit is the same: **one month from the move-out date in the undertaking** (Article 352(1) TBK).

**1. Enforcement without a judgment (ilamsız tahliye).** On the basis of the undertaking, the landlord can ask the enforcement office (icra dairesi) for eviction under Articles 272 et seq. İİK (12th Civil Chamber, E. 2024/2693, K. 2024/5602, 30.05.2024). The office serves an eviction order requiring the tenant to vacate and hand over the property within fifteen days (Article 272 İİK). The tenant can object within seven days, and the objection halts the proceedings (Article 274 İİK).

**2. Two options after an objection.** According to 3. HD, E. 2024/3875, K. 2025/759, 11.02.2025, the landlord can:

- ask the enforcement court (icra mahkemesi) to set the objection aside, if the undertaking was drawn up by a notary, its date and signature were certified by a notary, or the tenant has acknowledged it (Article 275 İİK);
- or file an action to annul the objection (itirazın iptali) at the civil court of peace (sulh hukuk mahkemesi) (Article 67 İİK).

Under decision 2024/2693, if the tenant's objection does not expressly deny the signature and date on a privately signed undertaking, they are treated as acknowledged and the enforcement court sets the objection aside.

**3. Direct lawsuit.** The landlord can also skip enforcement and sue for eviction at the civil court of peace (Article 4(1)(a) of the Code of Civil Procedure No. 6100, HMK), where the simple procedure applies (Article 316(1)(a) HMK).

## Is mediation mandatory?

Since 1 September 2023, Article 18/B(1)(a) of the Mediation in Civil Disputes Law No. 6325 (HUAK) reads:

Unofficial translation:

> a) Disputes arising from a tenancy relationship, excluding the provisions on the eviction of leased immovable property by enforcement without a judgment under Law No. 2004.

Original Turkish text:

> a) Kiralanan taşınmazların 2004 sayılı Kanuna göre ilamsız icra yoluyla tahliyesine ilişkin hükümler hariç olmak üzere, kira ilişkisinden kaynaklanan uyuşmazlıklar.

On the wording of the law, **enforcement proceedings** (Articles 272 et seq. İİK) do not require prior mediation. A direct **eviction lawsuit** at the civil court of peace, however, is a dispute arising from the tenancy, so you must apply to the mediation office before filing.

For an action to annul the tenant's objection after enforcement, the point is disputed. The following is an interpretation of the statutory text; no Court of Cassation decision ruling directly on this question was found for this article. In one case that was read (3. HD, E. 2024/1931, K. 2025/780), the court of first instance and the regional court of appeal dismissed such an action because no mediation had taken place; the Court of Cassation upheld the result on another ground (no legal interest in the appeal). In the author's assessment, this action is an ordinary court case outside the exception for enforcement without a judgment, so applying for mediation first is the safer course. How a court would decide is not certain.

From the application to the mediation office until the final report, limitation is suspended and time limits for bringing the claim (hak düşürücü süre) do not run (Article 18/A(15) HUAK). The mediator must finish within three weeks of appointment, extendable by at most one week in compelling cases (Article 18/A(9)). For eviction based on the landlord's own need, the 3rd Civil Chamber held that mediation may only be requested once the right to sue has arisen (E. 2025/1495, K. 2025/3048, 26.05.2025). That decision concerns Article 350 TBK. In the author's assessment, for an undertaking to vacate, too, the least risky course is to apply after the move-out date and within the one-month period.

## Practical points for landlords living abroad

**Getting the undertaking.** An undertaking that meets the statute is signed after the keys are handed over, on its own date, with a specific move-out date and the tenant's signature. In the author's assessment, a handover record and a separate date on the undertaking weaken a later claim that it was signed together with the lease. If the tenant agrees, having the date and signature certified by a notary opens the enforcement court route if the tenant objects (Article 275 İİK).

**Deadlines.** If you do not live in Turkey, the one-month period is easy to miss. Note the move-out date and instruct your representative before it arrives, either to start enforcement or to apply for mediation. If you sign a new lease with the tenant after the undertaking, you may no longer be able to rely on it, given decision 2026/2877.

**Power of attorney.** A lawyer in Turkey runs the enforcement or court case under a power of attorney, which you can give at a Turkish consulate; see the power of attorney section in [selling property in Turkey from abroad](/en/publications/selling-property-in-turkey-from-abroad). Parties can attend mediation in person, through legal representatives or through lawyers (Article 15(6) HUAK). The mediator will also contact you personally even if you have a lawyer (Article 18/A(7)), so provide a current phone number and e-mail address.

**Family home.** If the tenant is married and the spouse has notified you that the flat is the family home, have both spouses sign any later undertaking.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does the landlord have to act on the undertaking?

One month from the move-out date in the undertaking (Article 352(1) TBK). This applies to enforcement and to a lawsuit.

### Is an undertaking signed on the same day as the lease valid?

The law requires it to be given after handover. If it is proven that it was given before, it is invalid (3. HD 2025/2741). The tenant who makes this claim bears the burden of proof (HGK 2017/975, K. 2021/1108).

### Is mediation required before eviction?

Not for enforcement proceedings (Article 18/B(1)(a) HUAK). Yes for a direct eviction lawsuit. For an action to annul the tenant's objection it is disputed; mediating first is safer.

### Does the tenant's spouse have to sign?

Before a family-home notice, the tenant's signature is enough. After it, you need both signatures or the other spouse's express consent (3. HD 2025/3374).

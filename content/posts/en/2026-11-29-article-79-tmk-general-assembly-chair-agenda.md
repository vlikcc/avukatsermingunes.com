Article 79 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sets the procedure at the general assembly (genel kurul) of an association (dernek). Once the meeting is opened, the members elect a chair, as many vice-chairs as needed and secretaries to run it, and only items on the agenda (gündem) are discussed. If at least one tenth of the members present ask in writing for a topic to be discussed, it must be added to the agenda.

## Article 79 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **5. Meeting procedure**
>
> Article 79 – After the general assembly meeting has been opened, a chair and a sufficient number of vice-chairs and secretaries are elected to run the meeting.
>
> Only the items on the agenda are discussed at the general assembly meeting. However, topics that at least one tenth of the members present at the meeting request in writing to be discussed must be placed on the agenda.
>
> (Repealed third paragraph: 4/11/2004 – Law No. 5253, Art. 38)

Original Turkish text:

> **5. Toplantı usulü**
>
> Madde 79- Genel kurul toplantısının açılışından sonra, toplantıyı yönetmek üzere, bir başkan ve yeteri kadar başkan vekili ile yazman seçilir.
>
> Genel kurul toplantısında yalnız gündemde yer alan Maddeler görüşülür. Ancak, toplantıda hazır bulunan üyelerin en az onda biri tarafından görüşülmesi yazılı olarak istenen konuların gündeme alınması zorunludur.
>
> (Mülga üçüncü fıkra: 4/11/2004-5253/38 md.)

As of November 2026, the amendment table on mevzuat.gov.tr lists Article 79 only in the row for Law No. 5253 (in force 23 November 2004); it has not changed since (latest law in the table: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, paragraph 1 determines who runs the meeting and how many people are elected for this; paragraph 2 ties the meeting to its agenda and requires topics to be added when at least one tenth of the members present ask for it in writing. The third paragraph mentioned in the reasoning dealt with the attendance of a government commissioner; it was repealed by Article 38 of the Associations Law No. 5253 (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Commentary

### From opening to electing the presiding board

Article 79 starts after the opening; what happens before is set out in the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği). The list of members entitled to attend, drawn up by the board of directors, is kept at the venue. Members show an official identity document and sign next to their name to enter; anyone who does not show ID or sign is not admitted (Regulation Art. 15(1)). If the quorum is reached, this is recorded in minutes and the chair of the board of directors, or a board member he or she appoints, opens the meeting (Art. 15(2)). The assembly then elects the presiding board (divan heyeti): the chair, vice-chairs and secretary (Art. 15(3)).

### The chair's role and the minutes

The chair of the presiding board is responsible for running the meeting and keeping order (Regulation Art. 15(5)). In elections for the association's organs, voters show their ID to the presiding board and sign the attendance list (hazirun listesi) (Art. 15(4)). The topics discussed and decisions taken are recorded in minutes signed jointly by the chair and the secretaries. At the end, the minutes and other documents go to the chair of the board of directors, who keeps them and must hand them to the newly elected board within seven days (Art. 15(6)).

### Adding an item to the agenda

The one-tenth threshold is counted against the members present at the meeting, not all members of the association. A request for an extraordinary meeting, by contrast, needs one fifth of the members ([Article 75 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-75-tmk-extraordinary-general-meeting-association)). The request must be in writing; an oral proposal is not enough. A decision on a topic that was neither on the agenda nor added in this way breaches the law, and a member who did not vote for it, or was not present, can ask the court to annul it (Article 83 TMK). Board members who hold a general assembly in breach of the law or the statute also face an administrative fine (Associations Law No. 5253, Art. 32(1)(b)).

## Points for members living abroad

If the association holds the meeting electronically, the system must also allow the presiding board to be formed, votes to be cast, members to speak and motions to be submitted; every procedural rule for in-person meetings applies there too (Regulation Art. 15/A). A written request to add an agenda item can then be submitted as a motion through the system. Electronic meetings are covered in more detail in the post on [Article 73 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-73-tmk-association-general-assembly).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who chairs the general assembly of a Turkish association?

A presiding board elected after the opening: a chair, vice-chairs and secretaries (Article 79(1) TMK).

### Can members add an item to the agenda at the meeting?

Yes, if at least one tenth of the members present ask for it in writing (Article 79(2) TMK).

### Who signs the minutes of the general assembly?

The chair of the presiding board and the secretaries, jointly (Associations Regulation Art. 15(6)).

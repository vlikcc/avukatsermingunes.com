Article 43 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) opens the section on the death register (ölüm kütüğü) and says that death notifications follow "the relevant laws". Who reports a death, when and where is set out in the Population Services Law No. 5490 (Nüfus Hizmetleri Kanunu), not in the Civil Code. As of November 2026 the duty to report lies with public bodies and officials, and the period is ten days (Law No. 5490, Article 31(1)).

## Article 43 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **C. Death register**
>
> **I. Notification of death**
>
> Article 43 – Notifications concerning deaths are made in accordance with the relevant laws.

Original Turkish text:

> **C. Ölüm kütüğü**
>
> **I. Ölümün bildirilmesi**
>
> Madde 43- Ölümlere ilişkin bildirimler ilgili kanun hükümlerine göre yapılır.

As of November 2026, Article 43 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31/10/2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article corresponds to Article 41 of the former Civil Code No. 743. The notification periods in the old article were dropped because they were laid down in the Population Law No. 1587 and did not belong in the Civil Code (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly). Law No. 1587 is no longer in force; Law No. 5490 has replaced it.

## Explanation

Article 43 is a referral provision. The rules that apply are in the chapter "Death and absence" of Law No. 5490 (Articles 31–34).

Article 31(1) lists who must report a death: in towns, the authorities competent to issue burial permits; in villages, the public doctor or health facility, or failing them the village head (muhtar); in hospitals and care homes, the management; in military units, the designated officers; in natural disasters, officials appointed by the district governor; and in criminal cases and accidents, the public prosecutor. They report the death within ten days of the event to the General Directorate or the civil registry office (nüfus müdürlüğü). For Turkish missions abroad, the ten days run from the date they learn of the death.

A death in Türkiye is reported to the registry office where it occurred; if that place is unknown, where the body was found; if the death occurred in a vehicle, where the body was taken out (Article 31(3)). Late reports are accepted if they are based on records of public health institutions or other official documents (Article 31(2)).

If someone has died but is still shown as alive in the register, the registry office draws up the death record when a document proving the death is submitted. Without a document, the statement is investigated and the record is entered on the order of the district governor (Article 33(1)).

Notification does not create the legal effects of death; they arise at the moment of death (Article 28 TMK). The entry serves as proof: birth and death are proved by the entries in the civil register (Article 30 TMK). Registration where no body is found is governed by Article 44, registration of a declaration of absence by Article 45.

## Practical points for people living abroad

When a Turkish citizen dies abroad, the death is reported by handing the document issued by the local authority to the Turkish mission (Article 36(1) of the Implementing Regulation on Civil Registration Services). The law sets no deadline for relatives; the ten days apply to the consulate. Article 68 of Law No. 5490, in its current wording, provides administrative fines only for officials who fail to report on time, not for relatives. Until the death is registered, the person appears alive in the Turkish register and a notary cannot issue a certificate of inheritance (mirasçılık belgesi). Documents, apostille and effects on the estate are covered in [registering a death abroad in Türkiye](/en/publications/death-certificate-turkey-register-death-abroad).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who reports a death in Türkiye?

The bodies listed in Article 31(1) of Law No. 5490: burial-permit authorities, hospitals, village health staff or the muhtar, the prosecutor in criminal cases, and the consulate abroad.

### Within how many days?

Ten days; in Türkiye from the death, for missions abroad from when they learn of it (Law No. 5490, Article 31(1)).

### What happens if a death is never reported?

The person stays registered as alive. Relatives can apply to the registry office with a document proving the death (Law No. 5490, Article 33(1)).

Article 56 of Turkish Law No. 5718 on Private International Law and International Civil Procedure (MÖHUK) sets out how an enforcement case (tenfiz) ends. The Turkish court declares the foreign judgment enforceable in full or in part, or rejects the request. It cannot change the foreign judgment or add anything to it. If you want to enforce only part of a German or Dutch judgment in Turkey, for example the maintenance order, this is the provision that allows it.

## Article 56 MÖHUK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **Decision**
>
> Article 56 – (1) The court may order the enforcement of the judgment in part or in full, or reject the request. This decision is written beneath the foreign court judgment, sealed and signed by the judge.

Turkish original:

> **Karar**
>
> MADDE 56 – (1) Mahkemece ilâmın kısmen veya tamamen tenfizine veya istemin reddine karar verilebilir. Bu karar yabancı mahkeme ilâmının altına yazılır ve hâkim tarafından mühürlenip imzalanır.

The article has not been amended since the law entered into force on 12 December 2007. As of November 2026, the amendment table of the current text in the Ministry of Justice’s UYAP Mevzuat system lists only Article 27 (amended by Law No. 7550).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the official reasoning, the article reproduces Article 40 of the former Law No. 2675 without change (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, Article 55 of the bill).

## Explanation

### Three possible outcomes

The court either enforces the whole foreign judgment, enforces part of it and rejects the rest of the request, or rejects the request altogether. Partial enforcement arises in two situations. The applicant may ask from the start for only part of the judgment to be enforced and must then identify that part in the petition (Article 52(1)(c) MÖHUK). Or the conditions of Article 54 MÖHUK are met for only part of the judgment.

The court does not review whether the foreign judgment is correct. The Grand Chamber of the Court of Cassation (Yargıtay) for the unification of case law has held that under the MÖHUK system the enforcement judge cannot examine the foreign judgment on the merits or review its lawfulness (E. 2010/1, K. 2012/1, 10.02.2012). The Assembly of Civil Chambers (Hukuk Genel Kurulu) repeated that Turkish law does not accept a “révision au fond” (E. 2017/52, K. 2021/671, 03.06.2021). Partial enforcement therefore does not mean rewriting the judgment: one part is accepted as it stands and another is not.

### What the decision contains

An enforcement decision is a judgment like any other. Its operative part must rule on each request clearly and without ambiguity (Article 297(2) of the Turkish Code of Civil Procedure, HMK). The court is bound by the relief sought and cannot award more or something different (Article 26(1) HMK). Court of Cassation decisions draw these consequences for enforcement cases:

- The foreign judgment is enforced as it stands. The court cannot add a limitation that is not in it, such as an end date for maintenance.
- Enforcement does not create a new obligation. Converting the foreign amount into lira and ordering the defendant to pay it to the claimant is wrong; the court should confine itself to declaring enforceability.
- An enforcement case is not an action for performance but in the nature of a declaratory action; where the claim succeeds, the claimant is awarded a fixed-rate attorney fee and the costs of the proceedings.

The case references are listed below under “Court of Cassation decisions”.

### The note beneath the foreign judgment

The law requires the decision to be written beneath the foreign judgment, sealed and signed by the judge, so that one document shows which foreign judgment was declared enforceable and to what extent. In a case on a judgment given in Germany, for example, the first-instance court ordered the note to be added once its own decision became final; the Court of Cassation corrected the judgment only on costs and otherwise upheld it (3rd Civil Chamber, E. 2022/8254, K. 2023/1129).

### Link with other provisions

A foreign judgment declared enforceable is enforced like a Turkish judgment; appeals and their effect on enforcement are governed by Article 57 MÖHUK. Article 56 applies by analogy to foreign arbitral awards (Article 61(2) MÖHUK). Why an enforcement decision is needed at all is explained in [Article 50 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-50-mohuk-enforcement-turkey); which court hears the case in [Article 51 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-51-mohuk-competent-court-turkey).

## Practical points if you live abroad

**A divorce judgment with several orders.** A German divorce judgment often also deals with custody, maintenance or a payment. According to the Assembly of Civil Chambers, a divorce is a constitutive judgment and recognition (tanıma) is enough for the Turkish civil registry; but if the judgment also contains an order that needs enforcement, such as damages, maintenance or the handing over of a child, the conditions for enforcement apply to that part (E. 2011/2-593, K. 2011/726, 30.11.2011). State clearly in the petition which part you want recognised and which part enforced; the court is bound by your requests.

**Recognition first, enforcement later.** Separate parts of a judgment can be brought before the court at different times. The 2nd Civil Chamber upheld the later enforcement of the matrimonial property part of a US judgment, limited to assets in Turkey, after the divorce itself had already been recognised (E. 2022/10884, K. 2024/1360, 29.02.2024).

**A costs order is a separate decision.** Litigation costs are sometimes fixed in a separate decision. The Assembly of Civil Chambers treated separate costs orders (Kostenfestsetzungsbeschluss; masraf tespit kararı) from a Düsseldorf patent case as ancillary to the main judgment and upheld the decision enforcing those orders alone (E. 2017/52, K. 2021/671). If you want the costs order enforced too, name it expressly in the petition; the court does not rule on what was not requested (Article 26 HMK). If the court failed to rule on something that was requested, a supplementary decision can be applied for within one month of service of the final decision (Article 305/A HMK).

**Currency.** The law has no express rule on this. In my view the claim is enforced in the currency stated in the foreign judgment, because enforcement does not change the judgment’s content. In a case on child maintenance awarded in Polish złoty, enforcement was upheld despite an objection to the currency; the Court of Cassation did not address that objection separately in its reasons (2nd Civil Chamber, E. 2023/9757, K. 2024/2701, 18.04.2024).

You do not need to travel to Turkey for the case; it can be conducted through an authorised representative. For general information, see [Private international law](/en/practice-areas/private-international-law).

## Court of Cassation decisions

**Yargıtay 2. Hukuk Dairesi, E. 2016/3127, K. 2016/4747, 10.03.2016**

The first-instance court had enforced the maintenance order in a foreign judgment and added that maintenance would end when the divorce became final. The Chamber reversed: in such cases the foreign judgment is recognised and enforced as it stands, the claimant had made no such request, and the judge cannot go beyond the request (Article 26 HMK). It also held that a request for recognition and enforcement is a single request, so the defendant is not entitled to an attorney fee because part of it was rejected.

**Yargıtay 2. Hukuk Dairesi, E. 2018/1939, K. 2018/15039, 20.12.2018**

The first-instance court had converted EUR 20,000 in damages awarded abroad into TRY 64,890 and ordered the defendant to pay it to the claimant. The Chamber held that enforcing the damages order was right, but the court should have confined itself to enforcement; on rectification (karar düzeltme) it reversed on this point as well.

**Yargıtay 3. Hukuk Dairesi, E. 2022/8254, K. 2023/1129, 12.04.2023**

In a case on a judgment from Hünfeld, Germany, the Chamber held that an enforcement case is declaratory rather than an action for performance, so a fixed-rate attorney fee for the claimant was correct. Failing to award the claimant the costs of the proceedings was an error; the Chamber corrected and upheld the judgment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can only part of a foreign judgment be enforced in Turkey?

Yes. Article 56 MÖHUK expressly allows partial enforcement. You can ask for only one part (Article 52(1)(c)); if the conditions are met for only one part, that part is enforced and the rest of the request is rejected.

### Can the Turkish court change the foreign judgment?

No. The judge does not review the judgment on the merits. The Court of Cassation has treated adding an end date to maintenance, and converting the amount into lira with a payment order, as grounds for reversal.

### Can the judgment be enforced before the enforcement decision is final?

The effect of an appeal on enforcement is governed by Article 57(2) MÖHUK: “An appeal to the Court of Cassation suspends enforcement.” The details belong to Article 57 rather than Article 56.

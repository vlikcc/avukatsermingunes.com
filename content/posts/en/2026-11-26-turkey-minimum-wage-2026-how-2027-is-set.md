Turkey's minimum wage (asgari ücret) for 2027 will be set by the Minimum Wage Determination Commission (Asgari Ücret Tespit Komisyonu) at the Ministry of Labour and Social Security. Its decision is final, is published in the Official Gazette (Resmî Gazete) and takes effect on the date the decision states (Article 39 of Labour Law No. 4857; Article 11 of the Minimum Wage Regulation). In an announcement dated 3 November 2026 the Ministry said the commission will meet in December with government, worker and employer representatives; it gave no exact date.

There is no figure for 2027 yet, and this article does not offer a forecast. It explains the rules in force as of November 2026 and the 2026 amount published in the Official Gazette.

## The legal basis of the minimum wage in Turkey

The core provision is Article 39 of the Labour Law. The Ministry sets the minimum limits of wages through the commission **at least once every two years**, for every employee working under an employment contract, whether or not they fall under the Labour Law. "At least" means the commission may also meet every year. The article continues:

> Komisyon kararları kesindir. Kararlar Resmi Gazetede yayımlanarak yürürlüğe girer.

(Unofficial translation: The commission's decisions are final. They enter into force upon publication in the Official Gazette.)

How the commission meets and works is set out in the Minimum Wage Regulation (Asgari Ücret Yönetmeliği, Official Gazette 1/8/2004, No. 25540; last amended Official Gazette 1/7/2022, No. 31883). Since 2018 the composition of the commission is no longer in the Labour Law: the paragraph of Article 39 that listed its members was repealed by Decree-Law No. 700, and the rule is now in Presidential Decree No. 1.

In the current text of the Labour Law on mevzuat.gov.tr, the last amendment listed is Law No. 7578 (in force 1/5/2026); it did not touch Article 39. No Official Gazette amendment to the regulation after 2022 could be found.

## Who sits on the commission?

Under Article 522(1)(f) of Presidential Decree No. 1, the commission has 15 members:

- government side: the Ministry's Director General of Labour or a deputy, the Director General of Occupational Health and Safety or a deputy, and one representative each of the statistics office TÜİK, the Ministry of Treasury and Finance and the Ministry of Trade,
- worker side: five representatives of the confederation of trade unions with the most members,
- employer side: five representatives of the employers' confederation with the most members.

The chair is one of the members, chosen by the Ministry. Article 8 of the regulation still lists the members under older institution names; the presidential decree is the current rule.

## How the commission decides

The commission meets when the Ministry calls it and works on the Ministry's agenda (Article 8 of the regulation). It needs at least ten members present to meet and decides by majority vote. In a tie, the side the chair voted with prevails (Article 522(1)(f)).

Because ten members are enough, the commission can meet even if one side stays away. That is what happened with the 2026 decision: according to the Official Gazette, the worker representatives did not attend and those present decided unanimously.

In setting the wage the commission considers the country's social and economic situation, cost-of-living indices for wage earners, the wages actually being paid and living conditions (Article 7). The minimum wage is set **per day** as a rule; for monthly, hourly or piece-rate pay it is converted accordingly (Article 6). The commission's deliberations are confidential, the chair announces the decision (Article 9), and the decision must give reasons based on the information gathered (Article 8).

## When does the decision take effect?

The wage set by the commission is published in the Official Gazette. Unless the decision names another date, it takes effect on the day of publication (Article 11 of the regulation as amended on 1/7/2022). So the date from which the 2027 amount applies will be whatever the decision itself says. For 2026 it was 1/1/2026.

## What is the minimum wage in Turkey in 2026?

The commission's decision of 23/12/2025 (No. 2025/1) was published in the Official Gazette of 26 December 2025 (No. 33119). It set the minimum wage for one normal working day at **1,101 TL** for 1/1/2026 to 31/12/2026, as a single national rate. The decision itself states only this gross daily amount.

The monthly figures come from the Ministry's announcement of 23/12/2025: 33,030 TL gross and 28,075.50 TL net. The social security institution SGK's 2026 table also gives 33,030.00 TL as the monthly lower limit of earnings subject to contributions.

That table shows one amount for the whole period from 1/1/2026 to 31/12/2026. No second commission decision for 2026 could be found; as of November 2026 the amount above applies.

## What depends on the minimum wage

An employer may not pay less than the wage set by the commission, and employment contracts or collective agreements cannot say otherwise. Nor may the minimum wage be reduced because of social benefits the employer provides (Article 12 of the regulation). An employer who does not pay the minimum wage, or pays less, faces an administrative fine for each employee and each month (Article 102(a) of the Labour Law). General information on Turkish employment law: [employment law](/en/practice-areas/employment-law).

The link to social security is direct. Under Article 82 of Law No. 5510, the lower limit of daily earnings used to calculate contributions and benefits is one thirtieth of the minimum wage, and the upper limit is nine times the lower limit (the factor was raised from 7.5 to 9 by Law No. 7566, in force 1/1/2026). For 2026 SGK gives 1,101.00 TL and 9,909.00 TL per day. When a new minimum wage takes effect, these limits change too.

## If you live abroad: buying back years worked abroad

The minimum wage also sets the lowest amount payable when you buy back periods worked abroad (yurt dışı borçlanması). Under Article 4 of Law No. 3201, the amount for each day is 45% of a daily earnings figure you choose between the lower and upper limits under Article 82 of Law No. 5510, **as they stand on the date of your application**. In 2026 the lowest daily amount is therefore 45% of 1,101.00 TL, which is 495.45 TL.

When the minimum wage is reset for 2027, the lower limit will change, and applications made in 2027 will use the new limit. The law also allows the President to set a different lower limit for buy-back purposes, but not below the minimum daily earnings subject to contributions. Conditions and application: [Turkish pension: buying back years abroad](/en/publications/turkish-pension-buy-back-years-abroad).

## Frequently asked questions

### When will Turkey announce the 2027 minimum wage?

According to the Ministry's announcement of 3 November 2026, the commission will meet in December. The announcement gives no exact date. The amount becomes binding when it is published in the Official Gazette.

### How many members does the minimum wage commission have?

Fifteen: five each from government, workers and employers (Article 522(1)(f) of Presidential Decree No. 1). It meets with at least ten members and decides by majority.

### What is the Turkey minimum wage 2026 per month?

Per the Official Gazette of 26/12/2025: 1,101 TL gross per day. Per the Ministry: 33,030 TL gross and 28,075.50 TL net per month.

Article 5 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sits in the Code's introductory part. It says that the general provisions of the Civil Code and of the Code of Obligations apply, where suitable, to all private law relations. In practice, when the statute governing a family, inheritance, property, commercial or employment matter is silent, the answer is often in the general part of the Turkish Code of Obligations.

## Article 5 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **C. General provisions**
>
> Article 5 – The general provisions of this Code and of the Code of Obligations apply, where suitable, to all private law relations.

Turkish original:

> **C. Genel nitelikli hükümler**
>
> Madde 5 - Bu Kanun ve Borçlar Kanununun genel nitelikli hükümleri, uygun düştüğü ölçüde tüm özel hukuk ilişkilerine uygulanır.

The article has not changed since the Code entered into force on 1 January 2002. As of November 2026, the amendment table of the current text in UYAP Mevzuat (recorded 31 July 2026) does not list Article 5; the last law in the table is Law No. 7589.

## Legislative reasoning

According to the official reasoning, Article 5 of the former Code gave the impression that the general provisions of the Code of Obligations applied only to "civil law relations". The new text therefore speaks of "private law relations" and states expressly that the general provisions can be applied in every branch of private law (source: reasoning of the bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly).

## Explanation

### Which Code of Obligations?

Today it is the Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098 (Türk Borçlar Kanunu, TBK). It entered into force on 1 July 2012 (Article 648 TBK) and repealed the old Code of Obligations No. 818 (Article 647 TBK). Under Article 646 TBK, the TBK is the fifth book of the Civil Code and supplements it. The general provisions are in the first part of the TBK, headed "General Provisions" (Articles 1–206): formation of contracts, mistake, fraud and duress (Articles 30–39), agency (Articles 40 et seq.), torts, performance, default and limitation.

### Specific rule first, general rule second

"Where suitable" sets an order. If the law deals with a question itself, that provision applies. Defects of consent in a will are a good example. Under Article 39 TBK, a party who made a contract by mistake, fraud or duress is deemed to have ratified it unless, within one year of discovering the mistake or fraud or of the duress ending, it declares that it is not bound or reclaims what it gave. For dispositions on death, Article 504 TMK has its own rule: the disposition is invalid, but becomes valid if the testator does not revoke it within one year. For a will, Article 504 TMK is therefore the rule to apply.

Where there is no specific rule, the general one applies. For a partition agreement between heirs (paylaşma sözleşmesi), the TMK only requires written form (Article 676(3)). If someone claims they signed it because of fraud or duress, Articles 30–39 TBK apply, as the TMK has no rule of its own on this. This is the author's assessment based on the wording of Article 5.

The article works both ways. General TMK rules such as good faith in exercising rights (Article 2), good faith as knowledge (Article 3) and the burden of proof (Article 6) also apply to commercial and employment relations. The Turkish Commercial Code describes itself as an integral part of the Civil Code and, in commercial matters with no commercial provision, refers to commercial custom and then to the general provisions (Article 1 TTK).

If the general provisions give no answer either, Article 1 TMK sets out which sources the judge turns to: [Article 1 Turkish Civil Code (TMK) – Sources of Law](/en/publications/legislation/article-1-turkish-civil-code-sources-of-law).

## What matters in practice if you live abroad

Article 5 only matters if Turkish law applies; where there is a foreign element, that is first decided under the Turkish Private International Law Act (MÖHUK). If Turkish law applies, you are most likely to meet the rule with documents you sign abroad and send to Turkey. If a partition agreement between heirs is challenged for mistake, fraud or duress, the claim is examined under the general provisions of the TBK; for contracts, keep the one-year period of Article 39 TBK in mind.

A petition filed with a court is not a contract. Under the Plenary Civil Chamber ruling below, withdrawing a lawsuit is a procedural act and cannot be undone because of a defect of consent. In a petition you sign at a consulate or for your lawyer in Turkey, "feragat" (waiver of the claim), "vazgeçme" (abandoning) and "davayı geri alma" (withdrawal of the action) have different consequences. For the procedure of an uncontested divorce in Turkey, see [Uncontested divorce in Turkey while living abroad](/en/publications/uncontested-divorce-turkey-living-abroad).

## Court of Cassation rulings

**Yargıtay (Court of Cassation), Plenary Civil Chamber (Hukuk Genel Kurulu), E. 2025/464, K. 2025/809, 10 December 2025**

After an uncontested divorce, the wife filed a petition within the appeal period saying she "abandons" her case; the husband consented, and the court treated the case as never filed (Article 123 HMK, the Code of Civil Procedure). The wife argued she had been deceived and that the petition was only meant to waive the appeal. The 2nd Civil Chamber had reversed, holding that Article 36 TBK (fraud) applied through Article 5 TMK and that the fraud claim had to be examined. The Plenary Chamber disagreed: withdrawing a case and consenting to it are procedural acts of the parties, and unless the HMK provides otherwise, procedural acts cannot be revoked for a defect of consent (citing Articles 188(2) and 311 HMK). The decision was by majority; the dissent would have had the court clarify the wife's intention under Article 31 HMK.

**Yargıtay, 9th Civil Chamber, E. 2025/9327, K. 2026/454, 21 January 2026**

On compensation after a fixed-term employment contract was terminated for just cause (Article 437 TBK), the Chamber, also referring to Article 5 TMK, found no gap in the law. A reduction was to be assessed not by analogy with Article 438 TBK but under the general rule in Article 52 TBK, through the reference in Article 114(2) TBK (unanimous).

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Article 5 of the Turkish Civil Code mean?

The general provisions of the TMK and TBK apply, where suitable, to all private law relations, including family, inheritance, commercial and employment law. A specific provision takes precedence.

### Which Code of Obligations does it refer to?

Since 1 July 2012, the Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098. The old Code No. 818 was repealed by Article 647 TBK.

### Can I undo a court petition because I was deceived?

Under Plenary Civil Chamber ruling 2025/809, withdrawing a case is a procedural act and cannot be revoked for a defect of consent. For waiver and acceptance of a claim, Article 311 HMK allows annulment. This is a single majority ruling.

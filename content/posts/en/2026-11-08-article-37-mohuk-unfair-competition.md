Article 37 of Turkey’s Law No. 5718 on Private International Law and International Civil Procedure (MÖHUK) decides which law governs claims arising from unfair competition (haksız rekabet) with a foreign element. The rule is the law of the country whose market is directly affected. If the conduct harms only the interests of one particular business, the law of the country where that business is established applies. You may come across this article if your business sells from Germany or the Netherlands to customers in Turkey, or if a competitor in Turkey has copied your product or advertised misleadingly.

## Article 37 MÖHUK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **Unfair competition**
>
> Article 37 – (1) Claims arising from unfair competition are governed by the law of the country whose market is directly affected by the unfair competition.
>
> (2) Where the unfair competition infringes exclusively the business interests of the injured party, the law of the country where that business has its place of business applies.

Turkish original:

> **Haksız rekabet**
>
> MADDE 37 – (1) Haksız rekabetten doğan talepler, haksız rekabet sebebiyle piyasası doğrudan etkilenen ülke hukukuna tâbidir.
>
> (2) Haksız rekabet sonucunda zarar görenin münhasıran işletmesine ilişkin menfaatleri ihlâl edilmişse, söz konusu işletmenin işyerinin bulunduğu ülke hukuku uygulanır.

The article has not changed since the law came into force on 12 December 2007; as of November 2026 the current text in the UYAP Mevzuat system carries no amendment note.

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning of the government bill, the article is new; the former Law No. 2675 had no equivalent, and the Swiss Private International Law Act served as a model. Unfair competition is a special kind of tort and so was given its own connecting factor. The “directly affected market” test was chosen because it protects competitors, consumers and the public alike. Paragraph 2 covers conduct aimed at a business before any market is affected; there the law of the place of business is treated as most closely connected with the interest harmed. (Source: reasoning of the bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, Article 36 of the bill.)

## Explanation

Article 37 is a special case of the general tort rule ([Article 34 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-34-mohuk-tort-accident-turkey)). What counts is not where the conduct took place but which market feels its effect. If a company based in Germany advertises misleadingly to consumers in Turkey, the Turkish market is directly affected and Turkish law applies.

Paragraph 2 is a narrow exception. If the conduct targets “exclusively” the interests of one business, for example by obtaining its trade secrets, the law of that business’s place of business applies.

Where Turkish law applies, the rules are in Articles 54–63 of the Turkish Commercial Code No. 6102 (TTK). Under Article 54(2) TTK, deceptive conduct and commercial practices that otherwise breach good faith and affect relations between competitors or between suppliers and customers are unfair and unlawful; Article 55 lists the main cases. The injured party may seek a declaration that the conduct is unfair, an injunction, removal of the resulting situation, damages if there is fault, and non-pecuniary damages where the Code of Obligations allows it (Article 56(1) TTK). These claims become time-barred one year after the claimant learns of them and in any event three years after they arise; if the conduct is also a crime with a longer limitation period, that period applies (Article 60 TTK). Limitation follows the law governing the claim ([Article 8 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-8-mohuk-limitation-periods)). As of November 2026, Articles 54–63 TTK carry no amendment note.

Infringement of intellectual property rights such as trade marks or patents falls under Article 23 MÖHUK ([Article 23 MÖHUK](/en/publications/legislation/article-23-mohuk-intellectual-property-turkey)); the same conduct can be both trade mark infringement and unfair competition. Cartels and abuse of a dominant position are covered by Article 38 MÖHUK.

## Points to note if you live abroad

If a business in Germany or the Netherlands sells online into several countries, one advertisement may affect several markets at once. Whether each market’s own law then applies to the harm on that market is disputed. The law contains no express rule on this, and I could not find a binding decision of the Court of Cassation (Yargıtay); applying each market’s law to the loss on that market is my own assessment. How a court would decide in a given case is uncertain.

Unlike Article 34(5) and Article 39(2), Article 37 does not allow a choice of law after the event. Whether the parties can choose a law for unfair competition claims is not expressly addressed.

Turkish courts’ international jurisdiction follows the domestic rules on territorial jurisdiction (Article 40 MÖHUK). In tort claims, the courts where the act was committed, where the damage occurred or may occur, and where the injured party is domiciled also have jurisdiction (Article 16 of the Code of Civil Procedure No. 6100, HMK). If you sue in Germany or the Netherlands, that court applies its own conflict rules; for questions of German or Dutch law, please consult a lawyer admitted in that country. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which law applies if a foreign company advertises misleadingly in Turkey?

If the Turkish market is directly affected, Turkish law applies wherever the company is based (Article 37(1) MÖHUK). Turkish unfair competition law is in Articles 54 ff. TTK.

### What is the limitation period for unfair competition claims in Turkey?

Under Turkish law, one year from knowledge and at most three years from when the claim arose (Article 60 TTK). If a foreign law applies, its limitation rules govern (Article 8 MÖHUK).

### Is unfair competition the same as trade mark infringement?

No. Intellectual property rights fall under Article 23 MÖHUK, unfair competition under Article 37 MÖHUK. The same conduct can be examined under both.

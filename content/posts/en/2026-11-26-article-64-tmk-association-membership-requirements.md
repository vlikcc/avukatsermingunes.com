Article 64 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sets out who may join an association (dernek) and how a membership application is decided. Every natural person with capacity to act, and every legal person, has the right to join; the board decides on a written application within thirty days at most. This matters to anyone who wants to join an association in Türkiye and to the boards that handle applications.

## Article 64 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **2. Requirements**
>
> Article 64 – Every natural person with capacity to act, and legal persons, have the right to become members of associations.
>
> A membership application, which shall be made in writing, shall be decided (...) by the board of the association within thirty days at most, and the result shall be notified to the applicant in writing. A member whose application is accepted shall be entered in the register kept for this purpose.

Original Turkish text:

> **2. Koşulları**
>
> Madde 64- Fiil ehliyetine sahip bulunan her gerçek kişi ile tüzel kişiler, derneklere üye olma hakkına sahiptir.
>
> Yazılı olarak yapılacak üyelik başvurusu, (...) dernek yönetim kurulunca en çok otuz gün içinde karara bağlanır ve sonuç yazıyla başvuru sahibine bildirilir. Başvurusu kabul edilen üye, bu amaçla tutulacak deftere kaydedilir.

Footnotes in the official text:

- Paragraph 1: Article 32 of Law No. 4963 of 30 July 2003 inserted the words “ile tüzel kişiler” (and legal persons) after “gerçek kişi” (natural person).
- “(...)” in paragraph 2: Article 38 of Law No. 5253 of 4 November 2004 removed the words “tüzükte başkaca bir düzenleme yoksa,” (unless the statute provides otherwise).

As of November 2026, the amendment table on mevzuat.gov.tr lists Article 64 only under Laws No. 4963 and No. 5253; it has not changed since 2004 (latest law in the table: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, no special conditions are required for membership; capacity to act is enough. Paragraph 2 sets out what happens after an application (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised). The reasoning refers to the original wording; legal persons were added in 2003.

## Explanation

### Capacity to act

A person has capacity to act (fiil ehliyeti) if they have the capacity to judge, are of age and are not under guardianship (kısıtlı) (Article 10 TMK). Majority begins at 18 or on marriage (Article 11); a minor aged 15 or over can be declared of age by a court (Article 12). When a legal person is a member, its chair of the board or the person it appoints to represent it votes on its behalf (Article 6, Associations Law No. 5253, Dernekler Kanunu).

Capacity to act is only the legal minimum. The statute can add further conditions; Article 4(1)(c) of the Associations Law requires the statute to set out the conditions and form of joining. If a member later loses one of these conditions, membership ends automatically ([Article 65 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-65-tmk-automatic-end-association-membership)).

### Special cases in the legislation

- Children's associations (çocuk dernekleri): minors aged 15 or over who have the capacity to judge may found or join a children's association with the written permission of their legal representatives. From age 12, minors may join with their legal representatives' permission but may not sit on the board or the audit board. Persons over 18 may not be founders or members (Article 3, Associations Law).
- Public sector: Article 3(2) of the Associations Law preserves restrictions in special laws for members of the armed forces and law enforcement and for civil servants. These restrictions are set out in separate laws and are not covered here.
- Foreigners: foreign natural persons with the right to settle in Türkiye (yerleşme hakkı) may join associations; this condition does not apply to honorary membership (Article 93 TMK).

### Application, decision, registration

The application must be in writing. The board decides within thirty days at most, informs the applicant in writing and enters the new member in the register. Under Article 32 of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği), the membership register records each member's identity details and the dates of joining and leaving. The association reports new members to the associations authority where it has its seat within 45 days of admission (Article 23, Associations Law).

Because the words “unless the statute provides otherwise” were removed in 2004, the thirty-day limit and the written notice now apply whatever the statute says.

### The general assembly's final decision (Article 80(1))

Under Article 80(1) TMK, the general assembly (genel kurul) has the final say on admission and expulsion. That is as far as the text of the Code goes: the board decides first, within thirty days, and the general assembly makes the final decision. Neither Article 64 nor Article 80 says how, or within what time, the matter reaches the general assembly after the board's decision. For the rule that no association can be compelled to admit members, see the post on [Article 63 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-63-turkish-civil-code-association-membership).

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

You can send a written application from abroad; the Code sets no requirement as to where it comes from. Since the result is sent to you in writing, give an address where you can be reached. The settlement condition in Article 93 TMK applies only to people who hold a foreign nationality alone.

## Frequently asked questions

### How old do you have to be to join an association in Türkiye?

The general rule is capacity to act, which normally means 18 (Articles 10 and 11 TMK). Children's associations have age limits of 12 and 15 (Article 3, Associations Law).

### How quickly must an association decide on my application?

Within thirty days at most, with written notice of the result (Article 64(2) TMK).

### Can a company join an association in Türkiye?

Yes, legal persons can be members (Article 64(1) TMK). The chair of its board or an appointed person votes for it (Article 6, Associations Law).

Turkish severance pay (kıdem tazminatı) has two conditions: you must have worked for the same employer for at least one full year, and the employment must end in one of the ways listed in Article 14 of Labour Law No. 1475. If you simply resign, you are generally not entitled to it. The exceptions are resignation for just cause, compulsory military service, retirement, meeting the pension conditions other than age (for example 15 years and 3600 contribution days), and, for women, leaving within one year of marriage.

This is the law as of November 2026. Only Article 14 of Law No. 1475 is still in force; Article 120 of Labour Law No. 4857 repealed the rest. According to the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr, the last amendment to Article 14 was made by Decree-Law (KHK) No. 700 in 2018.

## Severance pay conditions in 2026: has anything changed?

No. Provisional Article 6 of Law No. 4857 provides for a severance pay fund and says that, until a fund law takes effect, employees keep their rights under Article 14 of Law No. 1475. No such law has been passed. The amendment table of Law No. 4857 ends with Law No. 7578 (in force since 1 May 2026), which changed Article 74 and Additional Article 2, not severance pay. The Turkish press regularly reports on a possible fund. Until a law appears in the Official Gazette (Resmî Gazete), this does not affect your rights.

Severance pay is due "for each full year"; any further part of a year counts proportionally. So if you have not completed one full year, there is no entitlement. Service counts across all workplaces of the same employer, even if the contract ended and was signed again. If the business is transferred, the periods are added together (1475 Art. 14(2)). Severance pay is paid only once for the same period of service.

## Severance pay when the employer terminates

References in Article 14 are now read as references to Law No. 4857 (Provisional Art. 1). If the employer dismisses you under Article 25(II) of Law No. 4857, which covers conduct contrary to morality and good faith, no severance pay is due. Examples are theft, abuse of trust, or two consecutive working days of absence without permission or good reason.

Any other termination by the employer triggers severance pay. That includes dismissal with notice and summary dismissal for health reasons (Art. 25(I)), force majeure (III), or police custody or arrest (IV).

## Severance pay when you resign

A plain resignation gives no entitlement. In the following cases, however, you receive severance pay even though you leave yourself.

### Resignation for just cause (4857 Art. 24)

If your wages are not calculated or paid as the law or contract requires (Art. 24(II)(e)), if the employer sexually harasses you or attacks your honour, or if work stops for more than a week because of force majeure, you may terminate without notice. This termination gives you severance pay. Where the ground is conduct contrary to morality and good faith, you must act within six working days of learning of it and in any event within one year of the act (4857 Art. 26).

### Military service and retirement

An employee who leaves for compulsory military service (muvazzaf askerlik) receives severance pay. So does an employee who leaves to receive an old-age, retirement or invalidity pension or a lump-sum payment (toptan ödeme). You must then show that you are entitled to the pension or lump sum and that you have applied to your pension institution (1475 Art. 14(3)).

### 3600 days and 15 years: pension conditions other than age

Under Article 14(1)(5), an employee who meets every pension condition except age and resigns is entitled to severance pay. Which conditions apply depends on when you were first insured in Turkey:

- **First insured before 8 September 1999:** Provisional Article 81 of Law No. 506 applies. The best-known option is 15 years of insurance and 3600 days of invalidity, old-age and survivors' insurance contributions (Provisional Art. 81(C)). Paragraph (B) contains further options with 20 or 25 years of insurance and between 5000 and 5975 days.
- **First insured between 8 September 1999 and 30 April 2008:** Article 14 refers this group to Article 60 of Law No. 506. That article has been repealed, and the reference is treated as a reference to the corresponding provision of Social Security Law No. 5510 (Art. 104). Under its Provisional Article 9, the conditions other than age are 25 years of insurance and 4500 days, or 7000 days.
- **First insured on or after 1 May 2008:** Article 14(1)(5) does not deal with this group expressly. This point is disputed. What follows is a view put forward in the legal debate; there is no express statutory rule, and I could not verify a binding court decision on it in an official database for this article. Which view a court will take in a particular case is uncertain. Relying on the general reference rule in 5510 Art. 104, it is argued that the condition other than age in 5510 Art. 28 applies, which for employees is 7200 days.

Your contribution days and first insurance date appear in your SGK service record (hizmet dökümü).

### Women who resign after marriage

A female employee who leaves of her own wish within one year of the date of her marriage receives severance pay (1475 Art. 14(1)). The law grants this right only to women.

### Death of the employee

If the employment ends because the employee dies, the severance pay goes to the statutory heirs.

## How is severance pay calculated?

You receive 30 days' pay for each full year, and a proportional amount for any part year. An employment contract or collective agreement may give more than 30 days.

The basis is the last wage, plus money and benefits with a monetary value that the employee receives under the contract or the law (1475 Art. 14(11), read with 4857 Art. 32). Where pay varies, for example piece rates or commission, the wages paid in the last year are divided by the days worked in that year. If you earn the minimum wage, the last wage is again the basis; for how the minimum wage is set, see [Turkey's minimum wage: 2026 and how 2027 is set](/en/publications/turkey-minimum-wage-2026-how-2027-is-set).

By law, the annual amount of severance pay fixed by collective or individual contract may not exceed the maximum retirement bonus payable for one year of service to the highest-ranking civil servant (1475 Art. 14). This limit, known in practice as the severance pay ceiling (kıdem tazminatı tavanı), is set every six months by a circular of the Ministry of Treasury and Finance. I could not check the figure for 1 July to 31 December 2026 against the official circular, so I do not give it here.

If severance pay is paid late, the court awards the highest interest rate paid on deposits for the period of delay.

## Time limit and mandatory mediation

Claims for severance pay become time-barred after **five years**, whichever law governs them (4857 Additional Art. 3; in force since 25 October 2017). The period starts when the claim falls due (Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098, Art. 149); for severance pay, that is the date the employment ends.

Before going to court you must apply to a mediator (arabulucu); this is a procedural requirement (Labour Courts Law No. 7036, Art. 3(1)). A claim filed without mediation is dismissed on procedural grounds. You apply to the mediation office where the employer is domiciled or where the work was done (Art. 3(5)). The mediator must conclude within three weeks, extendable by one week at most where necessary (Art. 3(10)). From the application until the final report, the limitation period is suspended (Art. 3(17)). A party who fails to attend the first meeting without a valid excuse bears half of the other side's court costs, even if it wins the case (Art. 3(12); amended by Law No. 7531, in force since 14 November 2024).

## Severance pay if you worked in Turkey and now live abroad

If you worked in Turkey under Law No. 4857, living in Germany or the Netherlands today does not change your entitlement. The conditions and the calculation are the same.

Resigning in order to move abroad, however, is not one of the cases in Article 14. If that is your only reason for leaving, no severance pay is due. It is different if another case applies at the same time, for example if you have met the pension conditions other than age or are within one year of your marriage. The author's assessment: in that case, state the ground clearly in your resignation letter, as this makes it easier to prove later.

You can take part in mediation from abroad through your lawyer (7036 Art. 3(18)). Which Turkish court has jurisdiction for work done abroad is explained in [Article 44 MÖHUK – Employment disputes in Turkish courts](/en/publications/legislation/article-44-mohuk-employment-disputes-jurisdiction). If the employer has gone bankrupt, see [Bankruptcy in Turkey: creditor claims and employees](/en/publications/bankruptcy-in-turkey-creditor-claims-employees). The end of an employment relationship in Germany or the Netherlands is governed by the law of that country and is not covered here. General information: [Employment law](/en/practice-areas/employment-law).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I get severance pay in Turkey if I resign?

Generally not. You do if you resign for just cause (4857 Art. 24), for military service, for a pension or lump sum, after meeting the pension conditions other than age, or, as a woman, within one year of marriage.

### What is the 3600-day rule for Turkish severance pay?

If you were first insured before 8 September 1999, 15 years of insurance and 3600 contribution days allow you to resign without waiting for the pension age and still claim severance pay (1475 Art. 14(1)(5), 506 Provisional Art. 81(C)).

### Does it apply to people first insured after 2008?

For dismissal by the employer, resignation for just cause, military service and marriage, yes. The number of days required to leave without meeting the age condition is not set out expressly in the law; 7200 days is one view.

### What is the time limit for claiming Turkish severance pay?

Five years (4857 Additional Art. 3). Mediation is mandatory before a lawsuit, and the limitation period is suspended while it runs (7036 Art. 3(17)).

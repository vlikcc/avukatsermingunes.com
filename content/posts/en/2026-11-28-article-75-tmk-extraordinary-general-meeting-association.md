Article 75 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) governs the extraordinary general meeting (olağanüstü genel kurul) of an association (dernek). The board must call one when the board or the audit committee thinks it necessary or when one fifth of the members ask for it in writing; if the board does not call it, the civil court of peace, on the application of one member, appoints three members to do so. The article matters most to members who are in dispute with the board.

## Article 75 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **b. Extraordinary meeting**
>
> Article 75 – The general assembly is called to an extraordinary meeting by the board where the board or the audit committee considers it necessary or upon the written application of one fifth of the members of the association.
>
> If the board does not call the general assembly, the civil judge of peace, upon the application of one of the members, appoints three members to call the general assembly.

Original Turkish text:

> **b. Olağanüstü toplantı**
>
> Madde 75- Genel kurul, yönetim veya denetim kurulunun gerekli gördüğü hâllerde veya dernek üyelerinden beşte birinin yazılı başvurusu üzerine, yönetim kurulunca olağanüstü toplantıya çağrılır.
>
> Yönetim kurulu, genel kurulu toplantıya çağırmazsa; üyelerden birinin başvurusu üzerine, sulh hâkimi, üç üyeyi genel kurulu toplantıya çağırmakla görevlendirir.

As of November 2026, Article 75 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The reasoning notes that in some important and pressing situations an extraordinary meeting may be needed. The first paragraph sets out which organs and how many members can ask for one; the second lets the civil judge of peace appoint three members if the board fails to call the meeting (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### Who can ask, who calls

The board, the audit committee (denetim kurulu) or one fifth of the members can ask for an extraordinary meeting. The board always makes the call. Under Article 13(1)(b) of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği), the general assembly meets within thirty days in these cases. One fifth is counted on the association's membership; an association with a hundred members needs twenty signatures. An extraordinary meeting does not wait for the date the statute sets for the ordinary meeting ([Article 74 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-74-tmk-ordinary-general-meeting-association)).

The law requires the application to be in writing and sets no other form. State clearly what you want discussed, because the meeting may only deal with items on the agenda (Article 79(2) TMK). The fifteen-day notice period (Article 77), the quorum (Article 78) and the majorities (Article 81) apply to extraordinary meetings as well.

### If the board does not call it: the civil court of peace

Under the second paragraph, an application by one member is enough; the one-fifth threshold no longer applies. The judge appoints three members to call the general assembly. Under Article 15 of the Regulation, they take over the tasks that article gives the board, such as checking identity documents at the venue and opening the meeting. The same route is open when vacancies reduce the board below half its full size and the general assembly is not called within a month (Article 84(2) TMK).

By the express wording of the article, the competent court is the civil court of peace (sulh hukuk mahkemesi). This matter is not listed separately among the non-contentious matters in Article 382(2) of the Code of Civil Procedure No. 6100 (HMK), and there is no specific rule on which court has territorial jurisdiction; this should be checked for the individual case before applying. In the author's view, the point at which the board "does not call" the meeting can be measured against the thirty-day period in the Regulation; the Act itself sets no time limit.

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

Living in Germany or the Netherlands does not stop you from signing the one-fifth application or applying to the civil court of peace; the law makes no distinction by place of residence. To be able to prove later that the application was made in writing and signed, you may prefer to send the signed original by registered post with acknowledgement of receipt or through a notary; this is a practical suggestion, not a form the law requires. You can also make the court application through a lawyer in Türkiye to whom you give a power of attorney. At the meeting itself you vote in person ([Article 69 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-69-tmk-voting-rights-association-general-assembly)); if the association has chosen to meet online, you log in with a secure electronic signature or two-factor authentication (Article 15/A of the Regulation).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is an extraordinary general meeting held?

When the board or the audit committee considers it necessary, or when one fifth of the members ask for it in writing (Article 75(1) TMK). Under the Regulation, the meeting is then held within thirty days (Article 13(1)(b)).

### What if the board refuses to call an extraordinary general meeting?

One member applies to the civil court of peace, which appoints three members to call the meeting (Article 75(2) TMK).

### Is a quorum required at an extraordinary meeting?

Yes. As at an ordinary meeting, an absolute majority of the members entitled to attend is needed; if it is not reached, no majority is required at the second meeting, but the number of members present may not be less than twice the total number of board and audit committee members (Article 78(2) TMK).

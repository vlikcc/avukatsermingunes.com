Article 78 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sets where the general assembly (genel kurul) of an association (dernek) meets and how many members must be present for the meeting to open, the quorum (toplantı yeter sayısı). Unless the statute says otherwise, it meets in the place where the association has its seat, with an absolute majority of the members entitled to attend. The article matters to boards running a meeting and to members who want to check whether it was valid.

## Article 78 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **4. Place of meeting and quorum**
>
> Article 78 – Unless the statute provides otherwise, general assembly meetings are held in the place where the association has its seat.
>
> The general assembly meets with the attendance of an absolute majority of the members entitled to attend, and in the case of amendment of the statute or dissolution of the association, with the attendance of two thirds; if the meeting is postponed because a majority was not reached, no majority is required at the second meeting. However, the number of members attending that meeting may not be less than twice the total number of members of the board of directors and the audit committee.
>
> A general assembly meeting may not be postponed more than once.

Original Turkish text:

> **4. Toplantı yeri ve toplantı yeter sayısı**
>
> Madde 78- Genel kurul toplantıları, tüzükte aksine hüküm olmadıkça, dernek merkezinin bulunduğu yerde yapılır.
>
> Genel kurul, katılma hakkı bulunan üyelerin salt çoğunluğunun, tüzük değişikliği ve derneğin feshi hâllerinde üçte ikisinin katılımıyla toplanır; çoğunluğun sağlanamaması sebebiyle toplantının ertelenmesi durumunda ikinci toplantıda çoğunluk aranmaz. Ancak, bu toplantıya katılan üye sayısı, yönetim ve denetim kurulları üye tam sayısının iki katından az olamaz.
>
> Genel kurul toplantısı, bir defadan fazla geri bırakılamaz.

As of November 2026, Article 78 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, paragraph 1 is meant to prevent drawbacks by having the meeting held at the association's seat unless the statute says otherwise; paragraph 2 sets the quorum in detail; paragraph 3 bars postponing the meeting more than once (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Commentary

### Place of meeting

The law does not say “at the association's premises” but “in the place where the association has its seat”. Read literally, the meeting may also be held in a hall in the same place. The statute may name another place. Board members who hold the meeting outside the place of the seat or the place named in the statute face an administrative fine, and the court may also annul a meeting held in breach of the law or the statute (Associations Act No. 5253, Art. 32(1)(b)).

### Quorum to meet and majority to decide

To open, the meeting needs an absolute majority of the members entitled to attend, and two thirds for an amendment of the statute or dissolution. The board draws up the list of members entitled to attend, and the list is kept at the venue (Associations Regulation, Dernekler Yönetmeliği, Art. 14(1), 15(1)). If the quorum is reached, this is recorded in minutes and the meeting is opened; if not, the board also records that in minutes (Regulation Art. 15(2)).

Once the meeting is open, decisions are taken by an absolute majority of the members present, and by two thirds of those present for an amendment of the statute or dissolution (TMK Art. 81). Keep the two numbers apart.

### Second meeting

If the first meeting lacks a quorum, no majority is needed at the second; the wording sets no separate figure here for an amendment of the statute or dissolution. The only floor is twice the total number of members of the board of directors and the audit committee (denetim kurulu). The day, time and place of the second meeting are already given in the first notice, and the two meetings are at least seven and at most sixty days apart (Regulation Art. 14(1)). For the notice rules, see the post on [Article 77 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-77-tmk-association-general-assembly-notice).

Whether substitute members count towards this floor is disputed. The law does not settle it expressly, and no binding court decision was found for this post. In the author's view, “total number of members” means the number of full members set in the statute; on that view, an association with five full board members (Art. 84) and three full audit committee members (Art. 86) needs at least 16 members at the second meeting. Which view a court would take in a given dispute is uncertain.

## Points for members living abroad

The general assembly of an association in Türkiye is held in person at the place of its seat unless the statute names another place. If the association has decided to hold the meeting electronically, the announcement says so, and all rules for in-person meetings apply to it as well (Regulation Art. 15/A; details in the post on [Article 73 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-73-tmk-association-general-assembly)). Voting by proxy is not possible; each member votes in person (TMK Art. 69(1)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the quorum for an association's general assembly in Türkiye?

An absolute majority of the members entitled to attend; two thirds for an amendment of the statute or dissolution (TMK Art. 78(2)).

### What happens if there is no quorum?

The meeting moves to the second date already given in the notice. No majority is needed then, but attendance must be at least twice the total number of members of the board of directors and the audit committee.

### Where is the general assembly held?

In the place where the association has its seat, unless the statute provides otherwise (TMK Art. 78(1)).

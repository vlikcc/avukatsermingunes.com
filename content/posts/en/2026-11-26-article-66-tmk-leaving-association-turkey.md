Article 66 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) governs leaving an association (dernek). No one can be compelled to remain a member, and every member may leave by giving the association written notice. This matters to anyone who wants to leave an association in Türkiye, including from abroad, and to the boards that process resignations.

## Article 66 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **2. By leaving**
>
> Article 66 – No one may be compelled to remain a member of an association. Every member has the right to leave the association, provided that they give notice (...) in writing.

Original Turkish text:

> **2. Çıkma ile**
>
> Madde 66- Hiç kimse, dernekte üye kalmaya zorlanamaz. Her üye (...) yazılı olarak bildirmek kaydıyla, dernekten çıkma hakkına sahiptir.

Footnote to “(...)” in the official text: Article 33 of Law No. 4963 of 30 July 2003 removed the words “altı ay önceden” (six months in advance).

As of November 2026, the amendment table on mevzuat.gov.tr lists Article 66 only under Law No. 4963; it has not changed since 2003 (latest law in the table: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, leaving an association, like joining one, depends on the person's own will; it is enough to give written notice of the wish to leave, and the written form is a condition of validity (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised). The reasoning refers to the original wording and still mentions notice six months in advance; that period was removed in 2003.

## Explanation

### One condition: written notice

The current text has no notice period. The only thing the Code requires is that the wish to leave is communicated to the association in writing. Leaving does not need the association's consent or a reason. Since the reasoning treats the written form as a condition of validity, a purely oral statement is not enough under this article.

Article 66 reflects Article 33(2) of the Turkish Constitution: “No one may be compelled to become or remain a member of an association.” For the freedom to join, see the post on [Article 63 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-63-turkish-civil-code-association-membership).

### Can the statute make leaving harder?

Article 4(1)(c) of the Associations Law No. 5253 (Dernekler Kanunu) requires the statute to set out the conditions and form of leaving. Article 66 TMK, on the other hand, makes leaving subject only to written notice.

> This point is disputed. The law does not say expressly whether the statute may attach conditions other than written notice to leaving, and no binding court decision could be verified for this post. In the author's assessment, a statute provision that in practice prevents a member from leaving cannot be applied, given the rule that no one may be compelled to remain a member; this is an opinion. How a court would decide in a given case is uncertain.

### What follows from leaving

- A member who leaves owes the dues for the time of membership (Article 70(1) TMK).
- They have no claim to the association's assets (Article 68(3) TMK).
- The association reports the end of membership to the associations authority within 45 days (Article 23, Associations Law) and records the leaving date in the membership register (Article 32, Associations Regulation, Dernekler Yönetmeliği).

The other ways membership ends are automatic termination ([Article 65 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-65-tmk-automatic-end-association-membership)) and expulsion (Article 67).

## What Turkish citizens living abroad should know

You can send the written notice from abroad; the Code does not specify where it must come from or how it must be sent. Choose a method that lets you prove later that the association received it, so that you can show your leaving date if dues for a later period are claimed. As of November 2026, the e-Devlet portal also lists a Ministry of the Interior service called “Dernek Üyeliği Sorgulama ve Sonlandırma” (checking and ending association membership), which requires logging in.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a notice period for leaving an association in Türkiye?

No. The words “six months in advance” were removed by Law No. 4963 in 2003; written notice is now enough (Article 66 TMK).

### What if the association does not accept my resignation?

Leaving is a right, and the Code does not make it subject to the association's consent (Article 66 TMK). No acceptance is needed.

### Do I still owe dues after leaving?

Yes, for the time you were a member (Article 70(1) TMK).

Article 77 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) governs how the general assembly (genel kurul) of an association (dernek) is called. The board of directors (yönetim kurulu) gives notice at least 15 days in advance, stating the day, time, place and agenda. The article matters to boards preparing a meeting and to members who want to check whether they were properly notified.

## Article 77 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **3. Notice of meeting**
>
> Article 77 – The general assembly is called to meet by the board of directors at least fifteen days in advance. For this purpose, the day, time, place and agenda of the meeting are communicated (...) to the members (...).
>
> The procedure for calling the meeting and matters relating to its postponement are governed by regulation.

Original Turkish text:

> **3. Toplantıya çağrı**
>
> Madde 77- Genel kurul, yönetim kurulunca, en az onbeş gün önceden toplantıya çağrılır. Bu amaçla toplantının günü, saati, yeri ve gündemi, (...) üyelere (...) bildirilir.
>
> Toplantıya çağrı usulü ve toplantının ertelenmesine ilişkin konular, yönetmelikle düzenlenir.

Footnote in the official text on the two “(...)”: “(4/11/2004 tarihli ve 5253 sayılı Kanunun 38 inci maddesinde; “yerel bir gazete ile ilân edilir ve aynı zamanda bir yazıyla” ibareleri madde metninden çıkarılmıştır.) hükmüne istinaden bu arada yer alan “yerel bir gazete ile ilân edilir ve aynı zamanda üyelere bir yazıyla” şeklindeki ibare, madde metninden çıkarılmıştır.” In short, the requirement to announce the meeting in a local newspaper and also write to each member was deleted.

As of November 2026, Article 77 appears in the amendment table on mevzuat.gov.tr only in the row for Law No. 5253 (in force 23 November 2004) and has not changed since (latest law in the table: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, paragraph 1 sets the rules for notice so that all members learn of the meeting in time and can attend; paragraph 2 leaves the details of notice and postponement to a regulation (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised). The reasoning refers to the original wording; newspaper notice and written notice have not been required since 2004.

## Commentary

### How is notice given?

The form of notice is now set by Article 14 of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği; last amended for this article in the Official Gazette of 23 January 2013, No. 28537). The board first draws up the list of members entitled to attend. Notice is given at least 15 days ahead by one of these means: an announcement in at least one newspaper or on the association's website, written notice, a message to the email address or phone number the member has provided, or local media. The statute must also state how and when the general assembly meets (Associations Act No. 5253, Art. 4(1)(d)), so check the statute too.

### The second meeting is announced in the same notice

The notice also states the day, time and place of a second meeting in case the first lacks a quorum. The two meetings must be at least seven and at most sixty days apart (Regulation Art. 14(1)). Members therefore know the date and place of the second meeting from the outset. The quorum itself is covered in the post on [Article 78 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-78-tmk-association-general-assembly-quorum).

### Postponement for other reasons

If the meeting is postponed for a reason other than lack of quorum, members are told, with the reasons, by the same method as the first notice. The new meeting must take place within six months of the postponement, and members are called again under the rules of paragraph 1. A general assembly meeting may not be postponed more than once (Regulation Art. 14(2) and (3); TMK Art. 78(3)).

### Why the agenda must be in the notice

The meeting discusses only items on the agenda, but an item must be added if at least one tenth of the members present ask for it in writing (TMK Art. 79(2)). Decisions taken by all members together without following the notice rules are valid (Art. 76). Annulment of decisions taken at a meeting that was not properly called can be sought within the time limits of Art. 83.

## Points for members living abroad

The Regulation lists the methods of notice with “or”. Unless the statute says otherwise, the wording allows the board to use, for example, only the association's website. A member living in Germany or the Netherlands may then simply not see it. It helps to give the association an up-to-date email address and phone number and to keep an eye on its website. Whether the meeting will be held electronically is also stated in the announcement (Regulation Art. 15/A; details in the post on [Article 73 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-73-tmk-association-general-assembly)).

A member who was not present can ask the court to annul (iptal) a decision that breaches the law or the statute within one month of learning of it, and in any case within three months of the decision (TMK Art. 83(1)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much notice is required for an association's general assembly in Türkiye?

At least 15 days (TMK Art. 77(1); Associations Regulation Art. 14(1)).

### How is notice of the general assembly given?

By an announcement in a newspaper or on the association's website, written notice, an email or message, or local media. The notice includes the day, time, place, agenda and the details of the second meeting (Regulation Art. 14(1)).

### Can items not on the agenda be discussed?

Only if at least one tenth of the members present ask for it in writing (TMK Art. 79(2)).

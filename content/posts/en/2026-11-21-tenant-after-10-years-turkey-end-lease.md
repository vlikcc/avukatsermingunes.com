Once the ten-year extension period (on yıllık uzama süresi) has run, a landlord in Turkey can end a residential lease without giving any reason. For a fixed-term lease, written notice that reaches the tenant at least three months before the end of an extension year is enough (Articles 347(1) and 348 of the Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098, TBK). The ten years start when the original lease term ends, not when the tenancy began, so on the wording of the law, with a one-year lease you can end it at the earliest at the end of year twelve.

This article is for landlords in Germany, the Netherlands or elsewhere in Europe who have let their flat in Turkey to the same tenant for many years. It covers how to work out the date, how to give notice and what happens if the tenant stays. If you or a relative need the flat, an [eviction claim for own use](/en/publications/evict-tenant-turkey-landlord-own-use) is a separate route and does not depend on the ten years.

As of November 2026, the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr shows that Articles 347 and 348 TBK have not changed since they came into force on 1 July 2012. The latest amendment to the TBK, Law No. 7589 (in force 31 July 2026), only changed Article 55. Mandatory mediation in tenancy disputes has applied since 1 September 2023 under Law No. 7445; the last amendment to the Mediation Law (HUAK) was made by Law No. 7531 (in force 14 November 2024). The press has reported proposals to amend Article 347 TBK; as of November 2026 none of them is in force.

## Article 347 TBK: what the ten-year extension period means

For residential and covered commercial premises, a fixed-term lease is extended for one year on the same terms unless the tenant gives notice at least fifteen days before the term ends, and the landlord cannot rely on the expiry of the term. The law limits this protection to ten years. Article 347(1), last sentence, and Article 347(2) TBK read (unofficial translation):

> However, at the end of the ten-year extension period, the landlord may terminate the lease without giving any reason, provided that notice is given at least three months before the end of each extension year following that period.
>
> In leases of indefinite duration, the tenant may terminate at any time, and the landlord after ten years have passed since the start of the tenancy, by giving notice in accordance with the general provisions.

The Turkish text:

> Ancak, on yıllık uzama süresi sonunda kiraya veren, bu süreyi izleyen her uzama yılının bitiminden en az üç ay önce bildirimde bulunmak koşuluyla, herhangi bir sebep göstermeksizin sözleşmeye son verebilir.
>
> Belirsiz süreli kira sözleşmelerinde, kiracı her zaman, kiraya veren ise kiranın başlangıcından on yıl geçtikten sonra, genel hükümlere göre fesih bildirimiyle sözleşmeyi sona erdirebilirler.

You do not need to show own use or any fault by the tenant. The lease ends through the landlord’s **notice of termination** (fesih bildirimi), not through a court judgment; a court claim only becomes necessary if the tenant does not move out.

## 10 or 12 years? Calculating a fixed-term lease

There are three steps:

1. The original term stated in the lease ends.
2. The ten-year extension period follows (ten one-year extensions).
3. The lease can be ended at the close of the next extension year; the notice must reach the tenant at least three months before that date.

Example: a one-year lease starts on 1 September 2015. The original term ends on 1 September 2016 and the ten-year extension period on 1 September 2026. The following extension year ends on 1 September 2027, so the notice must reach the tenant by 1 June 2027 at the latest. By then the tenant will have lived in the flat for twelve years. For a five-year lease, the same calculation gives 5 + 10 + 1 = 16 years.

This is where the common question “10 or 12 years?” comes from. The calculation above is the author’s literal reading of the words “each extension year following that period”. No court decision dealing directly with this calculation was reviewed for this article. In the author’s assessment, the safest approach for a landlord is to plan around this later date: a notice given for an earlier date risks having no effect on that date.

If your lease was signed before the TBK came into force on 1 July 2012, termination is still governed by the TBK (Article 1 of Law No. 6101 on the entry into force of the TBK). The two- and five-year transitional periods in its provisional Article 2 ended on 1 July 2014 and 1 July 2017. Because that provision refers to leases whose extension period had already “expired” when the TBK came into force, in the author’s assessment years before 2012 also count towards the ten.

## Leases of indefinite duration: ten years from the start

If the lease has no end date, the landlord may give notice under the general provisions once ten years have passed since the tenancy began (Art. 347(2) TBK). Unless local custom says otherwise, this means notice for the end of a six-month rental period, with three months’ notice (Art. 329). The periods are counted from the start of the lease; a longer notice period agreed in the lease applies instead (Art. 328(1)).

Example: an open-ended lease began on 1 March 2016, so the ten years ended on 1 March 2026. The six-month periods end on 1 March and 1 September each year. To end the lease on 1 March 2027, the notice must reach the tenant by 1 December 2026. If the notice period is missed, the notice takes effect for the next date (Art. 328(2)).

## Giving notice: writing, three months, notarial notice

For residential and covered commercial premises, notice of termination is valid only in **writing** (Art. 348 TBK). Oral notice does not meet this requirement; the law does not require a notary. In the author’s assessment, notice served through a Turkish notary (ihtarname) is the safest way, because it records the day the notice reached the tenant. Count the three months from receipt, not from posting.

The law does not prescribe the content of the notice. The author recommends stating the date of the lease, that you rely on Article 347 TBK and the date on which the lease will end. If the tenant is married and the non-tenant spouse has become a party to the lease by notifying you (family home, aile konutu), you must send the notice to each spouse separately (Art. 349(3)).

Whether a late notice under a fixed-term lease takes effect at the end of the next extension year is not stated in Article 347; the rule in Article 328(2) concerns open-ended leases. This point is disputed. In the author’s assessment, if you miss the deadline, the safest course is to give fresh notice for the following year.

## If the tenant does not leave: mediation and an eviction claim

If the tenant is still in the flat after the end date, the landlord brings an eviction claim (tahliye davası). All disputes arising from a tenancy go to the civil court of peace (sulh hukuk mahkemesi) (Art. 4(1)(a) of the Code of Civil Procedure No. 6100, HMK), under the simplified procedure (Art. 316(1)(a) HMK). Applying to a mediator (arabulucu) first is a precondition for the claim (Art. 18/B(1)(a) HUAK); a claim filed without it is dismissed on procedural grounds (Art. 18/A(2)). The mediator concludes the process within three weeks of appointment, extendable by at most one week where necessary (Art. 18/A(9)).

**Is there a deadline for the claim?** Unlike Articles 350 and 352 TBK, Article 347 sets no time limit for bringing the eviction claim after notice. This point is disputed: the law has no express rule, and no binding court decision on it was reviewed for this article. In the author’s assessment, tolerating the tenant’s stay for a long time and accepting rent without reservation may lead to an argument that the lease continues. The least risky course is to apply for mediation without delay after the end date.

Where the lease is in writing, the landlord can also ask the enforcement office for eviction within one month after the lease term ends (Art. 272 of the Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law No. 2004, İİK); this route is exempt from mandatory mediation (Art. 18/B(1)(a) HUAK). The law does not say expressly whether it can be based on notice under Article 347 TBK, and no decision on this was reviewed for this article. If the tenant objects within seven days, the enforcement proceedings stop (Art. 274 İİK). In the author’s assessment, mediation followed by a court claim is therefore the more predictable route.

## Can a tenant be evicted before the ten years are up?

Only on a ground set out in the law. These include own use by the landlord or close relatives (Art. 350 TBK), the needs of a new owner (Art. 351), the tenant’s written undertaking to vacate, two justified warnings about unpaid rent (Art. 352) and termination for non-payment of rent (Art. 315). For the undertaking, see [how to evict a tenant in Turkey: undertaking to vacate](/en/publications/how-to-evict-a-tenant-in-turkey-undertaking). The rules on ending a lease by court claim cannot be changed to the tenant’s disadvantage (Art. 354).

If your real aim is a rent closer to the market, eviction is not the only option: where a lease is renewed after five years, the court sets the rent taking comparable rents into account (Art. 344(3)). See [rent increase in Turkey: the legal limit](/en/publications/rent-increase-turkey-legal-limit).

## Landlords living abroad: practical points

You do not need to travel to Turkey to give notice. You can sign the written notice yourself and send it, or your lawyer in Turkey can serve it through a notary under your power of attorney. The lawyer must file a power of attorney drawn up or certified by a notary (Art. 76(1) HMK); you can grant it at a Turkish consulate in Germany or the Netherlands. In the author’s assessment, a power of attorney that expressly covers serving the notice, taking part in mediation and bringing the eviction claim avoids later arguments.

The parties can attend mediation in person, through legal representatives or through their lawyers (Art. 15(6) HUAK). The mediator also informs you directly even if you have a lawyer (Art. 18/A(7)), so give an up-to-date phone number and e-mail address.

Note the last date by which your notice must reach the tenant and instruct your representative well in advance, as service takes time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a landlord in Turkey end a tenancy after 10 years?

Yes. After the ten-year extension period, without giving a reason, by written notice at least three months before the end of an extension year (Art. 347(1), 348 TBK). If the tenant stays, mediation and an eviction claim follow.

### How are the 10 years calculated?

For a fixed-term lease, from the end of the original term; notice is given for the end of the following extension year. On the wording of the law, with a one-year lease that is the end of year twelve. For an open-ended lease, the ten years run from the start of the tenancy (Art. 347(2)).

### How long does eviction after 10 years take?

The end date follows from the statutory calculation, and the notice must arrive at least three months earlier. If the tenant stays, mediation takes three weeks, or at most four where necessary (Art. 18/A(9) HUAK). The law sets no time frame for the court proceedings.

After a divorce in Turkey, the parent who does not have custody pays child support (iştirak nafakası), contributing to the child’s care and education costs according to their means (Article 182 of the Turkish Civil Code, Türk Medeni Kanunu, TMK). Turkish law has no fixed amounts and no table. The judge sets the amount from the child’s needs and the parents’ ability to pay, adjusts it when circumstances change, and unpaid support is collected through enforcement proceedings, where the creditor can also ask for coercive detention of the debtor.

If the paying parent or the child lives in Germany or the Netherlands, the 2007 Hague Child Support Convention applies; Turkey has been a party since 2017. This article reflects the law as of November 2026, based on the current texts in UYAP Mevzuat, the Turkish Ministry of Justice legislation database. Spousal maintenance after divorce and the Constitutional Court ruling on it are covered separately: [Alimony in Turkey](/en/publications/alimony-turkey-indefinite-maintenance-ruling).

## What is iştirak nafakası and until what age is it paid?

Parents bear the costs of the child’s care, education and protection (TMK Art. 327(1)). After a divorce, the parent the child lives with carries these costs in practice and the other parent pays support. Who gets custody is a separate question, covered in [child custody after divorce in Turkey](/en/publications/child-custody-divorce-turkey).

Support can be ordered at three stages:

- **While the divorce case is pending:** the judge takes interim measures of their own motion, including for the children’s maintenance and protection (TMK Art. 169). This is interim support (tedbir nafakası).
- **In the divorce judgment:** the court sets child support together with the custody arrangement (TMK Art. 182).
- **Later:** the parent who actually looks after the minor can sue the other parent for support on the child’s behalf (TMK Art. 329(1)).

The duty lasts until the child comes of age. If an adult child is still in education, the parents must support them until the education ends, to the extent this can reasonably be expected of them (TMK Art. 328). In a chamber judgment adopted by the Assembly of Civil Chambers of the Court of Cassation (Yargıtay Hukuk Genel Kurulu), child support is said to end automatically when the child comes of age. In that case an adult student at an open high school had sued his father himself, and the Assembly held that a long period of schooling was no reason to refuse support (HGK, E. 2017/959, K. 2019/1131, 5 November 2019).

## How much child support is paid in Turkey?

There is no statutory figure. Under TMK Art. 330 the amount depends on:

- the child’s needs,
- the living conditions and ability to pay of both parents,
- any income of the child.

Support is paid monthly in advance (TMK Art. 330(2)). On request, the judge can decide in the judgment how much will be paid in future years according to the parties’ social and economic circumstances (TMK Art. 182, last sentence; Art. 330(3)). An annual increase clause is only added if it is requested.

Two recent decisions matter if you want school fees, school transport or health insurance listed as separate items. The 2nd Civil Chamber of the Court of Cassation held, in a case where a divorce protocol set out school fees, clothing and health insurance as separate obligations, that these costs belong within child support and that a single amount, clear enough to be enforced, must be set (E. 2025/8708, K. 2026/6569, 15 June 2026). The same chamber found that a protocol clause saying the parents would “share all school costs half and half” could not be enforced because the obligation was not determinable (E. 2026/3365, K. 2026/6507, 11 June 2026).

## Increasing child support

If circumstances change, the judge sets a new amount or ends the support on request (TMK Art. 331). The receiving parent can ask for an increase because of higher costs or the other parent’s higher income; the paying parent can ask for a reduction or termination, for example after a drop in income.

The case is heard by the family court (aile mahkemesi, Law No. 4787, Art. 4). For maintenance claims brought after the divorce, the court at the creditor’s place of residence has jurisdiction (TMK Art. 177). In the decisions reviewed, the increased amount applied from the date the claim was filed. In one child support increase case, the 2nd Civil Chamber held that the court was bound by the claimant’s request as to when the annual increase starts, and corrected the judgment accordingly (E. 2026/5699, K. 2026/7062, 23 June 2026).

The law does not say which index the annual increase follows. For spousal maintenance, the 2nd Civil Chamber has called indexation to the producer price index (ÜFE) its settled case law and replaced an increase based on the consumer price index (TÜFE) with ÜFE (E. 2024/6004, K. 2025/2090, 27 February 2025; E. 2025/2612, K. 2025/9515, 5 November 2025). In the child support decision 2026/7062 mentioned above, however, an increase tied to TÜFE was upheld and only its starting date corrected. We did not find a decision that clearly prescribes a particular index for child support, so no firm rule can be stated; the index you want should be named expressly in the claim.

## What happens if child support is not paid?

A support judgment is enforced in Turkey through enforcement based on a court judgment (ilamlı icra). The law gives maintenance some privileges:

- An appeal does not stop enforcement, and a stay of enforcement cannot be granted for maintenance judgments (Code of Civil Procedure, HMK Art. 350(1), 367(1); Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law, İİK Art. 36).
- The debtor’s salary can be garnished; at least a quarter of it is attached (İİK Art. 83). The employer must withhold the amount and send it to the enforcement office (İİK Art. 355).
- A creditor relying on a maintenance judgment can join another creditor’s attachment at the same rank without first going through the usual steps, except in bad faith (İİK Art. 101).
- If the debtor goes bankrupt, maintenance claims accrued in the year before bankruptcy rank first (İİK Art. 206).

If the debtor does not comply with the maintenance judgment, the court orders **coercive detention (tazyik hapsi) of up to three months** on the creditor’s complaint; once the debtor pays, he or she is released (İİK Art. 344). The complaint must be filed within three months of learning of the non-payment and in any case within one year (İİK Art. 347). The enforcement court where the enforcement proceedings were started has jurisdiction (İİK Art. 348). If the debtor has sued to end or reduce the support, the detention can be postponed until that case ends (İİK Art. 344). A detention order is no longer carried out two years after it becomes final (İİK Art. 354(2)).

## If a parent lives in Germany or the Netherlands

**Support claims before a Turkish court.** The law of the creditor’s habitual residence applies, that is the child’s (Article 19 of the Turkish Private International Law Act, MÖHUK). If the child lives in Turkey, Turkish law applies; if the child lives in Germany, even a Turkish court applies German law. More on this: [Article 19 MÖHUK – Maintenance](/en/publications/legislation/article-19-mohuk-maintenance-turkey).

**Coercive detention when the debtor lives abroad.** If the debtor lives elsewhere, the enforcement court can have him or her heard through judicial assistance; if the debtor cannot be brought before the court, it decides in his or her absence (İİK Art. 349). In the author’s view, detention can in practice only be carried out once the debtor enters Turkey, and the two-year limit (İİK Art. 354(2)) still runs. The debtor’s real estate and bank accounts in Turkey can be attached in the usual way.

**The 2007 Hague Convention.** Turkey acceded to the Convention on the International Recovery of Child Support and Other Forms of Family Maintenance with reservations and declarations (Law No. 6708, Official Gazette of 5 May 2016, No. 29703). According to the Hague Conference status table, it entered into force for Turkey on 1 February 2017; Germany and the Netherlands have been bound through the European Union’s approval since 1 August 2014. In short:

- It covers maintenance obligations arising from a parent-child relationship towards a person under 21 (Art. 2(1)(a)).
- Applications go through the Central Authority of the State where the applicant resides to the Central Authority of the other State (Art. 9). In Turkey this is the Ministry of Justice, Directorate General for Foreign Relations and European Union Affairs; in Germany the Bundesamt für Justiz; in the Netherlands the Landelijk Bureau Inning Onderhoudsbijdrage (LBIO).
- Available applications include recognition and enforcement of a decision, enforcement, a new decision where none exists, and modification of a decision (Art. 10(1)).
- For a creditor’s applications concerning child support for a person under 21, the requested State in principle provides free legal assistance (Art. 15).
- Between Contracting States it replaces, to the extent their scope overlaps, the 1973 Hague Convention on the recognition and enforcement of maintenance decisions and the 1956 UN (New York) Convention on the recovery abroad of maintenance (Art. 48, 49).

Turkey has reserved the right not to recognise or enforce maintenance arrangements, meaning authentic instruments or agreements registered with an authority (reservation under Art. 30). A maintenance instrument drawn up in Germany may therefore not be enforceable in Turkey through the Convention. A foreign court judgment can also be declared enforceable directly by a Turkish family court (tenfiz; MÖHUK Art. 50 ff.). For enforcement in Germany or the Netherlands and for the maintenance law of those countries, please consult a lawyer admitted there or the competent Central Authority.

## Frequently asked questions

### Until what age is child support paid in Turkey?

Until the child comes of age (TMK Art. 328(1)). An adult child still in education can claim support from the parents (TMK Art. 328(2)) and brings that claim personally.

### When can I apply to increase child support?

There is no waiting period. What matters is a change in the child’s needs or the parents’ circumstances (TMK Art. 331); the 2nd Civil Chamber has said a new assessment is then “always possible” (E. 2025/8708, K. 2026/6569).

### Can a father who does not pay child support go to prison in Turkey?

On the creditor’s complaint, coercive detention of up to three months can be ordered; it ends as soon as the debt is paid (İİK Art. 344).

### How do I get child support from a parent living abroad?

Between Turkey, Germany and the Netherlands the 2007 Hague Convention applies. You apply through the Central Authority of the country where you live (Art. 9).

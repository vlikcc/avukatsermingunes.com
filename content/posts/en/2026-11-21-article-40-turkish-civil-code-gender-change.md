Article 40 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sets the conditions for changing the sex recorded in the Turkish civil registry (nüfus kaydı). The procedure has two stages. First, a court grants permission for the gender change (izin). Then, once an official medical board report confirms that surgery in line with that permission has been carried out, the court orders the registry entry to be corrected. To obtain permission, the person must be at least 18, not married, and submit the medical board report described in the article.

## Article 40 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **2. In case of gender change**
>
> Article 40 – A person who wishes to change their sex may apply in person and ask the court to permit the gender change. However, permission may only be granted if the applicant has completed the age of eighteen and is not married, and documents, by an official medical board report from a training and research hospital, that they are of transsexual disposition and that the gender change is necessary for their mental health (…)³.
>
> If an official medical board report confirms that, in line with the permission granted, gender reassignment surgery appropriate to its purpose and to medical methods has been performed, the court orders the necessary correction in the civil registry.

Original Turkish text:

> **2. Cinsiyet değişikliğinde**
>
> Madde 40- Cinsiyetini değiştirmek isteyen kimse, şahsen başvuruda bulunarak mahkemece cinsiyet değişikliğine izin verilmesini isteyebilir. Ancak, iznin verilebilmesi için, istem sahibinin onsekiz yaşını doldurmuş bulunması ve evli olmaması; ayrıca transseksüel yapıda olup, cinsiyet değişikliğinin ruh sağlığı açısından zorunluluğunu (…)³ bir eğitim ve araştırma hastanesinden alınacak resmî sağlık kurulu raporuyla belgelemesi şarttır.
>
> Verilen izne bağlı olarak amaç ve tıbbî yöntemlere uygun bir cinsiyet değiştirme ameliyatı gerçekleştirildiğinin resmî sağlık kurulu raporuyla doğrulanması hâlinde, mahkemece nüfus sicilinde gerekli düzeltmenin yapılmasına karar verilir.

Footnote in the official text:

> ³ 20/3/2018 tarihli ve 30366 sayılı Resmî Gazete’de yayımlanan Anayasa Mahkemesinin 29/11/2017 tarihli ve E.: 2017/130, K.: 2017/165 sayılı Kararı ile, bu fıkranın ikinci cümlesinde yer alan “…ve üreme yeteneğinden sürekli biçimde yoksun bulunduğunu…” ibaresi iptal edilmiştir.

The footnote says that the Constitutional Court (Anayasa Mahkemesi, AYM), by its decision of 29/11/2017 (E. 2017/130, K. 2017/165; Official Gazette of 20/3/2018, No. 30366), annulled the words "…and is permanently unable to reproduce…". As of November 2026, the only entry for Article 40 in the amendment table on mevzuat.gov.tr is this annulment; the latest law in the table is Law No. 7589 (Articles 440 and 444, in force since 31/10/2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article was developed from a paragraph added to Article 29 of the former Civil Code by Law No. 3444 in 1988. Under the old rule, a person applied to court only after the change; the reasoning says this turned the court into a mere approval body and therefore requires prior court permission. It explains the in-person application by the strictly personal nature of the right and adds that this does not exclude representation by an agent. The reasons for the age and marriage conditions are given in the same text (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly). The reasoning belongs to the original version and therefore also mentions the reproduction requirement annulled in 2017.

## Explanation

### Stage one: permission

The court checks:

- An application in person. According to the reasoning, the case may still be filed through a representative.
- The applicant is at least 18.
- The applicant is not married.
- An official medical board report (resmî sağlık kurulu raporu) from a training and research hospital stating that the person is of transsexual disposition and that the change is necessary for their mental health.

Article 40 does not state which court has subject-matter or territorial jurisdiction. In the 2026 Constitutional Court decision cited below, the referring court was a civil court of first instance (asliye hukuk mahkemesi) hearing a case for correction of sex.

### Stage two: correcting the registry

If an official report confirms that surgery in line with the permission was carried out according to medical standards, the court orders the correction. The entry is changed only after the judgment becomes final; no registry entry may be corrected without a final court judgment (Population Services Law No. 5490, Article 35(1); TMK Article 39). A change of first name can be requested separately and requires good reason under TMK Article 27.

### Constitutional Court decisions

- Decision of 29/11/2017, E. 2017/130, K. 2017/165: annulment of the words on permanent inability to reproduce (Official Gazette 20/3/2018, No. 30366). Since then the report need not cover this point.
- Decision of the same day, E. 2015/79, K. 2017/164: paragraph 2 was reviewed on the merits and the request for annulment rejected (as summarised in the 2026 decision; published in the same Gazette).
- Decision of 26/3/2026, E. 2025/163, K. 2026/67: on a referral by the Antalya 13th Civil Court of First Instance, the Court held unanimously that the first sentence of paragraph 1 (court permission on an application in person) is not unconstitutional and rejected the referral (Official Gazette 30/6/2026, No. 33296). The decision explains that the referral concerning the second sentence of paragraph 1 was not examined for lack of reasons, and the one concerning paragraph 2 because of the 2017 decision on the merits (Constitution Article 152(4)).

As of November 2026, apart from the partial annulment of 2017, the article is in force in its original wording.

## Practical points for Turkish citizens living abroad

The sex recorded in the Turkish registry does not change automatically because of a change made abroad. Where the sex of a Turkish citizen has been changed abroad by a court decision, the obvious route is recognition of that decision in Türkiye (MÖHUK Article 58). This question is contested. Recognition requires that the decision is not manifestly contrary to Turkish public policy (kamu düzeni) (MÖHUK Article 54(1)(c) with Article 58); I do not cite a verified court decision on how a foreign decision that does not meet the conditions of TMK Article 40 (permission, age, marriage, report) is assessed. Whether changes made abroad by a declaration to an authority rather than by a court can be recognised at all is also contested. Which view a court will take in a given case is uncertain.

How your records in Germany, the Netherlands or elsewhere are changed is governed by the law of that country; please consult the competent authority or a lawyer admitted there. The Turkish procedure is closely tied to Türkiye because of its conditions and the reports from Turkish hospitals.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the requirements for a legal gender change in Turkey?

Age 18 or over, not married, an application in person, and an official medical board report from a training and research hospital on transsexual disposition and the necessity for mental health (TMK Article 40(1)).

### When is the Turkish registry changed?

When an official medical board report confirms surgery in line with the permission, the court orders the correction (TMK Article 40(2)); the entry is made once the judgment is final (Law No. 5490, Article 35(1)).

### What did the Constitutional Court decide on Article 40 in 2026?

By its decision of 26/3/2026 (E. 2025/163, K. 2026/67) it held the first sentence of paragraph 1 constitutional (Official Gazette 30/6/2026, No. 33296).

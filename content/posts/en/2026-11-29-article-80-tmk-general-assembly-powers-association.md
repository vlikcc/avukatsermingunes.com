Article 80 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sets out the duties and powers of the general assembly (genel kurul) of an association (dernek). It has the final say on admitting and expelling members, elects the association's organs, handles everything not assigned to another organ, supervises the other organs and may remove them at any time for just cause (haklı sebep). The article concerns board and audit committee members as well as members who want to question decisions taken in the association.

## Article 80 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **6. Duties and powers of the general assembly**
>
> Article 80 – The general assembly takes the final decision on admission to and expulsion from membership; it elects the organs of the association and handles matters not assigned to another organ of the association.
>
> The general assembly supervises the other organs of the association and may remove them from office at any time for just cause.

Original Turkish text:

> **6. Genel kurulun görev ve yetkileri**
>
> Madde 80- Genel kurul, üyeliğe kabul ve üyelikten çıkarma hakkında son kararı verir; dernek organlarını seçer ve derneğin diğer bir organına verilmemiş olan işleri görür.
>
> Genel kurul, derneğin diğer organlarını denetler ve onları haklı sebeplerle her zaman görevden alabilir.

As of November 2026, Article 80 on mevzuat.gov.tr is in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (latest law: No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, the article sets out the duties and powers of the general assembly as the association's highest organ; it supervises the other organs and may remove them at any time where there is just cause. Since it was considered natural that removed organ members can still exercise their contractual rights, no separate provision preserving those rights was included (Source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Commentary

### The statute and residual powers

Every association's statute must state the duties and powers of the general assembly and how it votes and takes decisions (Associations Law No. 5253, Art. 4(1)(e)). Article 80 TMK lists the duties the law gives the general assembly. Anything that neither the law nor the statute assigns to the board of directors or the audit committee stays with the general assembly.

### Decisions the Associations Law leaves to the general assembly

The Associations Law names the general assembly expressly for some decisions:

- Real estate: the association may buy or sell immovable property by a board decision, once the general assembly has authorised it (Art. 22).
- Pay and allowances: the general assembly sets any pay, allowances, travel costs and compensation for board and audit committee members who are not public officials, and the daily and travel allowances of members assigned to association work (Art. 13).
- Internal audit: the general assembly may carry out an internal audit itself, which does not relieve the audit committee of its duty (Art. 9(1)). The audit committee submits its report to the general assembly (Art. 9(2); Article 86(2) TMK).

Amending the statute and dissolving the association are also for the general assembly, each requiring two thirds of those attending (Articles 81 and 88 TMK). Its final say on admission and expulsion is covered in the posts on [Article 64 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-64-tmk-association-membership-requirements) and [Article 67 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-67-turkish-civil-code-expulsion-member).

### Removal and the agenda

The general assembly can remove organ members before their term ends. The agenda rule applies here too: the removal must be on the agenda or be added at the written request of at least one tenth of the members present (Article 79(2) TMK). If, after removals, the number of board members falls below half the full number because seats have become vacant, the remaining board members or the audit committee must call the general assembly within one month; if they do not, the magistrate (sulh hâkimi), at a member's request, appoints three members to call it (Article 84(2) TMK). A removed person who is a member of the association and considers the decision contrary to the law or the statute can ask the court to annul it, subject to the conditions and time limits in Article 83 TMK.

## Points for members living abroad

The article has no separate rule for members living abroad. If the board of an association in Türkiye has taken a decision about your membership against you, bear in mind that the final say lies with the general assembly, and that a board decision can only be challenged in court once the association's internal remedies have been used (Article 80(1), Article 83(2) TMK).

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the powers of the general assembly of a Turkish association?

The final decision on admission and expulsion, electing the organs, all matters not assigned to another organ, supervising the other organs and removing them for just cause (Article 80 TMK). It also decides on amending the statute, dissolution and authorising the purchase or sale of real estate.

### Can the general assembly remove the board of directors?

Yes, at any time for just cause (Article 80(2) TMK). The item must be on the agenda or properly added to it (Article 79(2)).

### Who decides to sell an association's property?

The board of directors, but only with the general assembly's authorisation (Associations Law Art. 22).

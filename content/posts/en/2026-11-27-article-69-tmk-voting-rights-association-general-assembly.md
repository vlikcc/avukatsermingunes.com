Article 69 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) sets out how members of an association vote in the general assembly (genel kurul). Every member has one vote and must cast it in person; honorary members have no vote. The rule matters to anyone who belongs to an association (dernek) in Türkiye, and especially to members who want to take part from abroad.

## Article 69 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **b. Right to vote**
>
> Article 69 – Every member has one vote in the general assembly; the member must cast the vote in person.
>
> Honorary members have no right to vote.

Turkish original:

> **b. Oy hakkı**
>
> Madde 69- Her üyenin genel kurulda bir oy hakkı vardır; üye, oyunu şahsen kullanmak zorundadır.
>
> Onursal üyelerin oy hakkı yoktur.

As of November 2026 the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr shows Article 69 in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (the latest law in the table is No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The reasoning is short: it follows from the principle of equality that each member has one vote (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised). The provision builds on the equality rule in Article 68.

## Explanation

### One member, one vote

The number of votes does not depend on the dues paid, on how long someone has been a member or on any office held. A founding member and a new member each have one vote. The statute must set out how the general assembly votes and takes decisions (Article 4(1)(e) of the Associations Law No. 5253), but it cannot change the one-vote rule. Decisions are taken by an absolute majority of the members present; amending the statute and dissolving the association require a two-thirds majority (Article 81 TMK).

### Voting in person

The second half of the sentence is what people ask about most: proxy voting is not allowed in the general assembly. A member cannot let another member or an outsider vote for them. Article 15 of the Associations Regulation (Dernekler Yönetmeliği) repeats the rule and requires members voting in elections for the association's bodies to show their identity document to the chair and sign next to their name on the attendance list.

Legal persons that are members vote through the chair of their board or the person they appoint as representative (Article 6 of the Associations Law).

### When a member may not vote

Under Article 82 TMK a member may not vote on a decision about a legal transaction or dispute between the association and the member, their spouse, ascendants or descendants. The same applies to the person voting for a legal person.

### Honorary members

Honorary members cannot vote and do not have to pay dues (Article 70(2)). Foreign nationals without a right of residence in Türkiye can only join as honorary members, since that condition does not apply to honorary membership (Article 93 TMK). They therefore have no vote in the general assembly.

## Practical points for people living abroad

If you live in Germany, the Netherlands or elsewhere in Europe and belong to a hometown or village association in Türkiye, you cannot give a relative a power of attorney to vote for you. A power of attorney made before a notary or at a consulate does not change this, because the rule is in the Code itself.

Two options remain. You attend in person, or the association has decided to hold the meeting electronically and you join online. Additional Article 2 of the Associations Law allows association matters to be handled electronically, and Article 15/A of the Associations Regulation allows general assemblies to be held on electronic systems approved by the IT Directorate-General of the Turkish Ministry of the Interior. Members log in with a secure electronic signature or two-factor authentication, and the board decision and the notice of meeting must say which format will be used. Members cannot demand an online meeting; it is the association's choice.

If a general assembly you did not attend took a decision that breaks the law or the statute, you can ask the court to annul it within one month of learning of it and in any event within three months of the decision (Article 83(1) TMK).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I vote by proxy at an association's general assembly in Türkiye?

No. Members must vote in person (Article 69(1) TMK; Article 15 of the Associations Regulation).

### Can a Turkish association hold its general assembly online?

Yes, if it chooses to. The meeting runs on systems approved by the Ministry of the Interior, and all the procedural rules for an in-person meeting apply (Article 15/A of the Associations Regulation).

### Can honorary members vote?

No (Article 69(2) TMK).

Articles 9 and 10 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) govern capacity to act (fiil ehliyeti). Article 9 says what it allows: acquiring rights and taking on obligations through your own acts. Article 10 lists the conditions: capacity of discernment, majority and not being under interdiction. Both are one-sentence provisions that belong together, so they are discussed in one post. Signing a contract, granting a power of attorney or renouncing an inheritance all require capacity to act. The Code was published in the Official Gazette (Resmî Gazete) of 8 December 2001, No. 24607, and entered into force on 1 January 2002.

## Articles 9 and 10 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **II. Capacity to act**
>
> **1. Scope**
>
> Article 9 – A person who has capacity to act can acquire rights and assume obligations through their own acts.

> **2. Conditions**
>
> **a. In general**
>
> Article 10 – Every adult who has capacity of discernment and is not under interdiction has capacity to act.

Original Turkish text:

> **II. Fiil ehliyeti**
>
> **1. Kapsamı**
>
> Madde 9 - Fiil ehliyetine sahip olan kimse, kendi fiilleriyle hak edinebilir ve borç altına girebilir.

> **2. Koşulları**
>
> **a. Genel olarak**
>
> Madde 10 - Ayırt etme gücüne sahip ve kısıtlı olmayan her ergin kişinin fiil ehliyeti vardır.

Neither article has changed since 1 January 2002. As of November 2026, the amendment table of the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr does not list Articles 9 or 10; the latest law in the table is Law No. 7589 (Articles 440 and 444, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

According to the reasoning, Article 9 corresponds to Article 9 of the former Code; the old formula "iktisaba da iltizama da ehildir" was replaced with "can acquire rights and assume obligations through their own acts". Article 10 corresponds to the former Article 10. The old text mentioned only majority and discernment; "not being under interdiction" was added as the third condition (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Turkish Grand National Assembly).

## Explanation

### Three conditions, all required

**Capacity of discernment (ayırt etme gücü).** Everyone has it who is not deprived of the ability to act reasonably because of young age, mental illness, mental weakness, intoxication or similar causes (Article 13 TMK). The law does not tie this to an age; what matters is the person's state at the time of the act.

**Majority (erginlik).** It begins on completing 18 years of age; marriage also confers majority (Article 11 TMK). A minor aged 15 or over can be declared of age by the court (Article 12 TMK).

**Not under interdiction (kısıtlı olmamak).** Interdiction (kısıtlama) is ordered by the court. The grounds are listed: mental illness or mental weakness (Article 405), prodigality, addiction, immoral lifestyle or mismanagement (Article 406), imprisonment (Article 407) and the person's own request (Article 408). Article 407 was amended by Law No. 7499 with effect from 12 March 2024: an adult serving a final prison sentence in a penal institution is placed under interdiction or given a curator (kayyım) at their own request; a person serving five years or more in total may be placed under interdiction even without a request, if this is needed to protect them or their assets.

### When a condition is missing

Persons without discernment, minors and persons under interdiction lack capacity to act (Article 14 TMK). The consequences differ:

- Acts of a person without discernment have no legal effect, subject to exceptions in the law (Article 15 TMK).
- Minors and persons under interdiction who have discernment cannot bind themselves by their own transactions without the consent of their legal representative. Consent is not needed for gratuitous benefits or for exercising strictly personal rights. They are liable for their torts (Article 16 TMK).

## Practical points if you live abroad

**Which law applies?** Legal capacity and capacity to act are governed by the person's national law (Article 9(1) MÖHUK, Turkish Private International Law Act No. 5718). A Turkish citizen's capacity to act is therefore decided under Turkish law even if the transaction takes place in Germany or the Netherlands. For dual nationals who are also Turkish, Turkish law applies (Article 4(1)(b) MÖHUK). Someone who lacks capacity under their national law but has it under the law of the country where the transaction is made is bound by the transaction, except for family and inheritance law transactions and rights in rem over immovable property in another country (Article 9(2) MÖHUK). More: [Article 9 MÖHUK – legal capacity](/en/publications/legislation/article-9-mohuk-legal-capacity).

**Powers of attorney.** Anyone granting a power of attorney at a consulate or notary, for example to sell property in Turkey or to conduct a case, must have capacity to act. If an elderly parent in Turkey has lost discernment due to serious illness, relying on an older power of attorney is risky; interdiction and the appointment of a guardian should be considered: [Guardianship for an elderly parent in Turkey](/en/publications/guardianship-elderly-parent-turkey).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between legal capacity and capacity to act?

Every human being has legal capacity, meaning the ability to hold rights (Article 8 TMK). Capacity to act is the ability to exercise them through your own acts, and requires the three conditions in Article 10.

### Can a 17-year-old sign a contract alone in Turkey?

If they have discernment, they can accept gratuitous benefits and exercise strictly personal rights; for transactions creating obligations they need the legal representative's consent (Article 16(1) TMK). If married or declared of age by a court, they are of full age (Articles 11, 12 TMK).

### Does a prison sentence lead to interdiction?

Under Article 407 as in force since 12 March 2024, an imprisoned adult is placed under interdiction or given a curator at their own request. With five years or more in total, interdiction is possible without a request if found necessary.

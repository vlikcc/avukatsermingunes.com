Article 71 of the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 (Türk Medenî Kanunu, TMK) covers the duties of association members other than paying dues. A member must follow the association's order and be loyal to it, and must avoid conduct that makes it harder or impossible to achieve the association's purpose. The provision concerns members of an association (dernek) in Türkiye and boards considering an expulsion.

## Article 71 TMK – text

Unofficial translation:

> **b. Other obligations**
>
> Article 71 – Members are obliged to comply with the order of the association and to show loyalty to the association.
>
> Every member is obliged to act in accordance with the association's purpose and, in particular, to refrain from conduct that hinders or prevents the achievement of that purpose.

Turkish original:

> **b. Diğer yükümlülükler**
>
> Madde 71- Üyeler, dernek düzenine uymak ve derneğe sadakat göstermekle yükümlüdürler.
>
> Her üye, derneğin amacına uygun davranmak, özellikle amacın gerçekleşmesini güçleştirici veya engelleyici davranışlardan kaçınmakla yükümlüdür.

As of November 2026 the current text on mevzuat.gov.tr shows Article 71 in its original wording; the amendment table has no entry for it (the latest law in the table is No. 7589, in force since 31 October 2026).

## Legislative reasoning

The reasoning calls these the member's "non-financial" (manevî) obligations. Because they are hard to list one by one, paragraph 1 sets a general rule and paragraph 2 names some specific duties that fall within it (source: reasoning of the government bill submitted to the Grand National Assembly, summarised).

## Explanation

### Duties beyond dues

The Code puts members' obligations in two articles: dues in Article 70 and everything else in Article 71. It does not define "the order of the association" (dernek düzeni); in the author's view the term covers the law, the statute and the decisions the association's bodies take in line with them. Loyalty is a broader standard for how a member behaves towards the association. Paragraph 2 names its clearest form: no conduct that hinders or prevents the association's purpose.

### A line the Code does not draw

> This point is disputed. What follows is the author's assessment; there is no express statutory provision on the scope of the duty of loyalty and no binding court decision. It is not certain which view a court would follow in a specific dispute.

The Code does not list what counts as disloyal. In the author's view the duty of loyalty does not stop a member from using rights the law gives them. Every member may take part in the association's activities and management (Article 68(2)); voting against a proposal, asking for an extraordinary meeting (Article 75) or suing to annul a general assembly decision that breaks the law or the statute (Article 83) should not be treated as disloyalty. The line runs between criticism and conduct that actually obstructs the association's purpose, and depends on the facts.

### Breach and expulsion

Article 71 does not set a sanction itself. A possible consequence is expulsion if the conditions in Article 67 are met: where the statute lists grounds for expulsion, a member expelled on those grounds cannot argue that they are unjustified; where the statute does not regulate expulsion, a member can only be expelled for just cause and may challenge the decision by arguing there was none. The general assembly has the final say on admission and expulsion (Article 80(1)). For expulsion and how to challenge it, see the post on [Article 67 TMK](/en/publications/legislation/article-67-turkish-civil-code-expulsion-member).

## Practical points for people living abroad

These duties do not depend on where the member lives. Social media posts about an association in Türkiye by a member living in Europe are judged by the same standard; the Code has no separate rule for them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a member be expelled for criticising the board?

The Code does not prohibit criticism, and every member has a right to take part in management (Article 68(2) TMK). Expulsion requires a ground listed in the statute or, if the statute is silent, just cause (Article 67).

### What does the duty to follow the association's order cover?

The Code does not define it (Article 71(1) TMK). In the author's view it means complying with the law, the statute and the decisions of the association's bodies taken in line with them.
